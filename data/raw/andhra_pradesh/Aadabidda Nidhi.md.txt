# Aadabidda Nidhi

## 1. Scheme Name

**Aadabidda Nidhi**

Also referred to as:

* **Aadabidda Nidhi Scheme**
* **Aadabidda Nidhi – Women’s Welfare Fund**

---

## 2. Scheme Status

**CURRENT_STATUS:** PROPOSED / UNDER REVIEW
**SOURCE_VERIFICATION_STATUS:** VERIFIED_PROPOSED_SCHEME — IMPLEMENTATION NOT CONFIRMED

Aadabidda Nidhi was announced as a proposed Andhra Pradesh women-welfare scheme with a promised benefit of **₹1,500 per month** for eligible women. However, the available 2026 reporting indicates that the scheme was still **under review and its implementation guidelines had not been finalized**. ([dailypioneer.com](https://www.dailypioneer.com/uploads/2025/epaper/september/vijayawada-english-edition-2025-09-27.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

The Government has therefore **not provided sufficient official evidence to classify it as an active 2026 beneficiary scheme**.

---

## 3. Objective

The proposed objective of Aadabidda Nidhi is to provide **regular financial assistance to eligible women in Andhra Pradesh** and support women's economic security and financial independence.

The scheme was included among the welfare commitments announced for women during the 2024 Andhra Pradesh election period. The documented proposed benefit was **₹1,500 per month**. ([dailypioneer.com](https://www.dailypioneer.com/uploads/2025/epaper/september/vijayawada-english-edition-2025-09-27.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

---

## 4. Target Beneficiaries

The publicly documented proposal identifies:

* **Women residing in Andhra Pradesh**
* Proposed age range: **18–59 years**

The ₹1,500/month benefit and 18–59 age range are repeatedly reported in available sources describing the proposal. ([orfonline.org](https://www.orfonline.org/research/cash-transfers-as-an-instrument-for-poverty-alleviation-and-womens-empowerment-in-india?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

However:

> **A final Government of Andhra Pradesh eligibility notification was not located confirming the complete beneficiary definition.**

Therefore, the RAG system must treat the age/category information as **proposed**, not as a deterministic current eligibility rule.

## 12. Current Implementation Status

The most important finding for the RAG database is that **Aadabidda Nidhi should not currently be treated like Thalliki Vandanam, Deepam 2.0 or NTR Bharosa**.

In September 2025, the reported Legislative Council discussion stated that:

* The scheme was **under review**
* Estimates were being prepared
* **Procedural guidelines had not yet been finalized**. ([dailypioneer.com](https://www.dailypioneer.com/uploads/2025/epaper/september/vijayawada-english-edition-2025-09-27.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

February 2026 reporting likewise described the scheme as still being examined, with no confirmed launch date. ([aimindia.in](https://aimindia.in/aadabidda-nidhi-scheme-ap-women/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Therefore, the RAG record must preserve the distinction between:

**announced/proposed scheme**

and

**implemented welfare scheme**.
