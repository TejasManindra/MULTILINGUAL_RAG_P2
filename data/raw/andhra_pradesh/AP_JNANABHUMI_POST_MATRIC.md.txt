## 2. Scheme Overview

The Andhra Pradesh Post Matric Scholarship programme provides
financial assistance to eligible students pursuing post-matric
education.

The current Jnanabhumi portal states that the scheme is implemented
on a saturation basis for eligible students belonging to:

- SC
- ST
- BC
- EBC (other than Kapu)
- Kapu
- Minority
- Differently Abled

The programme has two major components:

### RTF — Reimbursement of Tuition Fee

RTF provides reimbursement of eligible tuition and other approved
fees according to the applicable fee structure and Government Orders.

The current Jnanabhumi portal describes RTF as full fee reimbursement
for eligible students.

### MTF — Maintenance / Food and Hostel Assistance

MTF provides annual maintenance assistance:

- ITI: ₹10,000 per student
- Polytechnic: ₹15,000 per student
- Degree and above: ₹20,000 per student

The current Jnanabhumi portal states that MTF is intended for food
and hostel expenses.

The amount is credited to the mother's bank account where the
applicable implementation rules require mother-account payment.
If the mother is deceased or unavailable, payment may be made to
the natural guardian according to the applicable rules.


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

Eligible students pursuing post-matric courses.

The current Jnanabhumi portal identifies the following beneficiary
categories:

1. Scheduled Caste (SC)
2. Scheduled Tribe (ST)
3. Backward Classes (BC)
4. Economically Backward Classes (EBC), other than Kapu
5. Kapu
6. Minority
7. Differently Abled

The programme is intended for students pursuing eligible
post-matric education in recognised institutions.


## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### Course Level

Eligible courses include:

- ITI
- Polytechnic
- Degree
- Degree and above-level courses

The current Jnanabhumi portal specifically describes Polytechnic,
ITI and Degree & above courses.

Intermediate is not treated as a post-matric scholarship course
under this current scheme record.


### Institution

Eligible students may study in:

- Government colleges/institutions
- Aided colleges/institutions
- Eligible private colleges
- Institutions affiliated to State Universities / Boards

The institution must satisfy the applicable recognition,
affiliation and Jnanabhumi requirements.


### Attendance

The current Jnanabhumi portal states:

75% aggregate attendance is mandatory for release of scholarships.

In addition, G.O.Ms.No.87 dated 20-11-2024 states that:

- From AY 2024-25, RTF for students other than SC students is
  released to college accounts according to the revised guidelines.
- From AY 2025-26, RTF for students other than SC students is
  subject to FRS attendance in colleges.
- SC RTF continues to be released to student accounts according to
  the applicable Government of India guidelines.

Therefore attendance must be represented as a current operational
condition, with the exact release mechanism differentiated by
category/year.


### Family Income

The current Jnanabhumi scheme page specifies:

Total family annual income:
Less than or equal to ₹2.50 lakh.

This is the principal income threshold currently displayed by the
Jnanabhumi portal.


### Landholding

The current Jnanabhumi portal specifies that family landholding
must be within the prescribed limits:

- Less than 10 acres wet land, OR
- Less than 25 acres dry land, OR
- Less than 25 acres combined wet and dry land.

Important:
The portal's wording should be preserved exactly in the eligibility
engine because older application forms contain a different
presentation of the land condition.


### Government Employment / Pension

No family member should be a government employee or pensioner.

Exception:
Sanitary workers are exempt from this exclusion irrespective of
salary/recruitment, subject to the applicable certification process.


### Four-Wheeler

No family member should own a four-wheeler.

Exemptions include:

- Taxi
- Tractor
- Auto

as specified by the Jnanabhumi eligibility rules.


### Urban Property

The current Jnanabhumi portal states that the family is eligible
where it owns no property or less than 1500 square feet of built-up
area in urban areas.

Therefore:

Urban property threshold:
Less than 1500 sq ft.


### Income Tax

No family member should be an income-tax payer.


### Local Nativity

Jnanabhumi application documentation requires local nativity
information.

The application form records AP local nativity under Article 371-D.

The form specifies that the student should have studied at least
four years in Andhra Pradesh out of seven years of total study up
to the qualifying course for the indicated local-nativity test.

This condition should be evaluated according to the applicable
admission/local-status rules rather than being treated as a simple
residence-duration rule for every student.


## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

The current Jnanabhumi portal identifies the following categories
as ineligible:

1. Students studying in Private Universities / Deemed Universities.
2. Students pursuing Correspondence / Distance Education.
3. Students admitted under Management / Spot Quota.

Additional non-eligibility conditions include failure to satisfy
the prescribed:

- Income criteria
- Land criteria
- Vehicle criteria
- Urban-property criteria
- Government employment/pension criteria
- Income-tax criteria
- Attendance requirements
- Category/caste requirements
- Institution/course requirements


## 6. Benefits

### RTF — Reimbursement of Tuition Fee

Eligible students receive reimbursement of the approved fees under
the applicable fee structure.

The current Jnanabhumi portal describes full fee reimbursement
covering:

- Tuition Fee
- Special Fees
- Other Fees
- Examination Fees

subject to the applicable Government-approved fee structure.


### MTF — Maintenance / Food and Hostel Assistance

Annual amounts currently displayed by Jnanabhumi:

| Course | Annual MTF |
|---|---:|
| ITI | ₹10,000 |
| Polytechnic | ₹15,000 |
| Degree & above | ₹20,000 |

The MTF is intended for food and hostel expenses.


### MTF Payment

MTF is credited to the mother's bank account under the applicable
implementation mechanism.

If the mother is deceased or unavailable, the amount may be credited
to the natural guardian according to the applicable rules.


### RTF Payment — Current Version

G.O.Ms.No.87 dated 20-11-2024 modified RTF disbursement:

From AY 2024-25:
RTF for students other than SC students is released to the
respective college accounts.

From AY 2025-26:
RTF for students other than SC students is released to college
accounts subject to FRS attendance.

For SC students:
RTF continues to be released to student accounts according to
applicable Government of India guidelines.

The Jnanabhumi application documentation requires bank-account

For mother-account payment under the applicable RTF/MTF mechanisms,
the mother's bank account must satisfy the required Aadhaar-seeding/
authentication conditions.


## 8. Application Process

### Fresh Application

The Jnanabhumi process generally follows:

1. Student obtains the scholarship application information/form
   through the college.
2. Student provides required personal, caste, income, education and
   bank details.
3. College verifies the student's information.
4. College submits the application through Jnanabhumi.
5. Student verifies the application details.
6. Required authentication/biometric verification is completed.
7. Welfare department verifies eligibility.
8. Scholarship is sanctioned according to the applicable rules.
9. RTF/MTF is released through the applicable payment mechanism.


### Renewal

For renewal:

1. College enters current-year admission information.
2. Previous examination/hall-ticket information is updated.
3. Student verifies the application.
4. Required authentication is completed.
5. Welfare department processes the renewal.


### Institutional Role

Colleges are responsible for:

- Registration
- Application submission
- Fee details
- Student verification
- Attendance information
- Examination/result information
- Other required Jnanabhumi updates

The Social Welfare Directorate is the nodal authority for issuing
timelines/guidelines and monitoring implementation.

- Income-tax status

## 12. Related / Linked Government Programs

### Jagananna Vidya Deevena

RTF component of the post-matric scholarship framework.

Provides reimbursement of approved educational fees.

### Jagananna Vasathi Deevena

MTF component.

Provides annual maintenance assistance for eligible students.

### JnanaBhumi

The integrated Andhra Pradesh education/scholarship portal through
which applications and institutional processing are carried out.

### Post-Matric Scholarships for Different Welfare Categories

The same broad post-matric framework is administered across multiple
welfare departments.

The eligibility category must therefore be retained in the RAG.

### Overseas Scholarship Schemes

Ambedkar Overseas Vidya Nidhi and other overseas scholarships are
separate schemes.

Do not merge their income/age/qualification rules with the
post-matric scholarship.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. The current official name is "Post Matric Scholarships (RTF/MTF)"
   on Jnanabhumi.

2. Jagananna Vidya Deevena and Jagananna Vasathi Deevena are the
   commonly used names of the RTF and MTF components.

3. Do not create duplicate RAG entries unless historical scheme
   tracking is required.

4. The current portal should be preferred for current eligibility
   values.

5. G.O.Ms.No.87 dated 20.11.2024 must be incorporated into the
   current eligibility/payment logic.

6. RTF payment mechanics changed from AY 2024-25.

7. FRS attendance becomes specifically important for non-SC RTF
   releases from AY 2025-26.

8. SC RTF has a different disbursement treatment because of the
   Government of India funding component.

9. The RAG must distinguish RTF from MTF.

10. The RAG must distinguish beneficiary eligibility from the actual
    approved fee amount.

11. The ₹2.50 lakh income threshold should not be replaced with older
    district-page figures from unrelated or historical scholarship
    implementations.

12. The land threshold should be stored exactly as the current
    Jnanabhumi portal presents it.

13. Do not copy eligibility rules from Pre-Matric Scholarships,
    Overseas Scholarships or Differently-Abled-only schemes.

14. Course and institution eligibility must be checked before
    determining benefit.

15. Academic-year deadlines should be stored as separate metadata
    rather than permanently hard-coded.
