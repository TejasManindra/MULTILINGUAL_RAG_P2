## 2. Scheme Overview

NTR Bharosa Pension Scheme is the Government of Andhra Pradesh's social security pension programme for eligible vulnerable and economically disadvantaged beneficiaries.

The scheme provides monthly financial assistance to specified beneficiary categories including old-age persons, widows, persons with disabilities, weavers, toddy tappers, fishermen, single women, transgender persons, traditional cobblers, Dappu artists, ARTPLHIV beneficiaries and specified medical-condition categories.

The scheme name was restored to NTR Bharosa Pension Scheme through G.O.Ms.No.43 dated 13.06.2024.

The enhanced pension rates ordered through the 2024 Government Order became applicable from July 2024, with the ₹4,000 category receiving arrears as specified in the Government Order.

Current operational status is supported by the official NTR Bharosa dashboard, which records pension disbursements for September 2026.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### Common Eligibility Conditions

The official NTR Bharosa portal specifies the following common conditions

- The proposed beneficiary shall be from a BPL family holding a white ration card.
- The beneficiary shall be a local resident of the district.
- The beneficiary shall not be covered under another pension scheme.

These common conditions should be interpreted together with the category-specific eligibility conditions and applicable exclusion factors.


### Age

#### Old Age Pension

- Male or female.
- 60 years of age or above.
- The official portal also describes the beneficiary as destitute, meaning having little or no means of subsistence and no family or relative to depend upon.

#### Weavers Pension

- 50 years of age or above.
- The official portal describes the beneficiary as destitute.

#### Widow Pension

- 18 years and above, according to the current official NTR Bharosa eligibility page.

#### Disabled Pension

- No age limit.
- Minimum 40% disability according to the official NTR Bharosa eligibility page.

#### Toddy Pension

- 50 years and above.
- The official portal refers to members of Toddy Cooperative Societies or individual tappers under the Tree for Tappers scheme and contains a historical reference to completion of 50 years as on 01.02.2009.

The historical date should not be interpreted as a newly imposed 2026 age cutoff.

#### ART  PLHIV Pension

- No age limit stated on the current official eligibility page.
- Six months continuous treatment on Anti-Retroviral Therapy (ART).

#### CKDU Pension

- No age limit stated.
- Patients undergoing continuous kidney dialysis for Chronic Kidney Disease of Unknown Etiology (CKDU).

#### Transgender Pension

- 18 years of age or above.

#### Fishermen Pension

- 50 years of age or above.

#### Traditional Cobblers Pension

- Above 40 years.

#### Single Women Pension

Married women who are separateddeserted

- Separation period must be more than one year as on the date of pension sanction.

Unmarried women
- 30 years and above in rural areas.
- 35 years and above in urban areas.
- Must have no support of the family according to the official eligibility page.

#### Dappu Artist Pension

- Above 50 years.

## 6. Benefits

The current benefit structure is based primarily on G.O.Ms.No.43 dated 13.06.2024.

### ₹4,000 per month

The Government Order specifies ₹4,000 per month for

- Old Age Pension
- Widow Pension
- Weaver Pension
- Toddy Tapper Pension
- Fishermen Pension
- Single Women Pension
- Traditional Cobblers Pension
- Transgender Pension
- ART  PLHIV Pension
- Dappu Artists Pension
- Artist pensions

### ₹6,000 per month

The Government Order specifies ₹6,000 per month for

- Persons with disabilities
- Multi-deformity leprosy persons

The disabled pension eligibility page specifies a minimum 40% disability for the general disabled pension category.

### ₹15,000 per month

The Government Order specifies ₹15,000 per month for

- Paralysis confining the person to a wheelchair or bed
- Severe muscular dystrophy cases
- Accident victims under the specified pension category

The Government Order describes this section under fully disabled persons pensions.

### ₹10,000 per month

The Government Order specifies ₹10,000 per month for specified chronic conditions including

- Bilateral Elephantiasis Grade 4
- Kidney transplant
- Liver transplant
- Heart transplant
- CKDU not on dialysis with serum creatinine greater than 5 mg
- CKDU not on dialysis with estimated GFR below 15 ml
- CKDU not on dialysis with small contracted kidney
- CKDU undergoing dialysis
- Sickle cell disease
- Thalassemia
- Severe haemophilia below the specified factor 89 threshold

The exact medical certification requirements for each condition should be verified separately before use in the deterministic eligibility engine.

### Historicaltransition payment

The 2024 Government Order states that the ₹4,000 category was payable from 1 April 2024 and was to be disbursed from July 2024 with three months' arrears.

This historical arrears provision should not be interpreted as the normal current monthly pension amount.

Current ordinary benefit frequency
Monthly.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. This document is intended for a government-scheme RAG system and not as a legal determination of eligibility.

2. The eligibility engine must not make a final government eligibility decision.

3. Where current authoritative sources conflict, the system should surface the conflict rather than silently selecting a value.

4. The 2024 Government Order is the primary source for the enhanced pension amounts.

5. The live NTR Bharosa portal confirms current operational status but contains legacy information in some sections. Legacy values must not override the later 2024 Government Order.

6. Category-specific medical pensions require medicaldocument verification and should not be evaluated using only generic demographic information.

8. Final eligibility, sanction and pension continuation are determined by the competent Government of Andhra Pradesh authorities based on the applicable rules and administrative verification.
