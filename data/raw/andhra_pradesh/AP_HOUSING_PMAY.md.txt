## 2. Scheme Overview

The Andhra Pradesh housing programme is implemented through the
A.P. State Housing Corporation Limited (APSHCL) in coordination with
Government of India and Government of Andhra Pradesh housing
programmes.

APSHCL states that its objective is to assist BPL families in
constructing permanent/pucca houses.

The current housing framework includes:

### Rural

Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana-Gramin (PMAY-G)

PMAY-G is the Government of India's rural housing programme for
eligible houseless households and households living in kutcha or
dilapidated houses.

### Urban

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)

PMAY-U 2.0 provides housing assistance to eligible EWS/LIG/MIG
families living in urban areas.

PMAY-U 2.0 covers multiple implementation verticals including:

- Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC)
- Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP)
- Affordable Rental Housing
- Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)

### Andhra Pradesh State Housing Programme

Navaratnalu–Pedalandariki Illu was an Andhra Pradesh flagship
housing/house-site programme launched under the previous state
government.

It included house-site distribution and housing construction in
layouts and own-site locations.

That historical programme must be retained separately from the
current PMAY-U 2.0/PMAY-G eligibility engines.


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

### PMAY-G

Primary target:

Rural households that are:

- Houseless, or
- Living in kutcha/dilapidated housing,

and satisfy the PMAY-G eligibility/selection criteria.

Beneficiary selection is based on the prescribed deprivation/
housing-status criteria and government verification.


### PMAY-U 2.0

Primary target:

Urban families belonging to:

- EWS
- LIG
- MIG

who satisfy the scheme's housing-ownership and other eligibility
conditions.

Current PMAY-U 2.0 income categories:

EWS:
Annual household income up to ₹3 lakh.

LIG:
Annual household income above ₹3 lakh and up to ₹6 lakh.

MIG:
Annual household income above ₹6 lakh and up to ₹9 lakh.


### Historical Pedalandariki Illu

Historical target beneficiaries included eligible weaker-section
families/households receiving:

- House-site pattas
- Houses in government-developed layouts
- Houses on own sites
- PMAY-linked housing assistance


## 4. Eligibility Criteria

# A. PMAY-G — Rural

### Rural Residence

The household must reside in a rural area.

### Housing Condition

PMAY-G is intended for households that:

- Do not have a shelter, or
- Live in a kutcha house, or
- Live in a dilapidated house,

subject to the complete PMAY-G eligibility/selection process.

### Pucca House

A household owning a pucca house is generally excluded.

### Number of Rooms

The official PMAY-G eligibility questionnaire asks whether the
house has more than two rooms.

A household with more than two rooms is excluded under the
PMAY-G eligibility filter.


### Motorised Vehicle

Households owning a motorised three-wheeler or four-wheeler are
excluded under the PMAY-G eligibility filter.

### Agricultural Machinery

Households owning mechanised three/four-wheeler agricultural
equipment are excluded.


### Kisan Credit Card

Households having a Kisan Credit Card with a credit limit of
₹50,000 or above are excluded under the PMAY-G eligibility filter.


### Government Employment

A household with a government employee is excluded under the
PMAY-G eligibility filter.


### Non-Agricultural Enterprise

Households having a non-agricultural enterprise registered with the
Government are excluded under the eligibility filter.


### Monthly Income

If any household member earns more than ₹15,000 per month, the
household is excluded under the PMAY-G eligibility filter.


### Income Tax

If any household member pays income tax, the household is excluded.


### Professional Tax

If any household member pays professional tax, the household is
excluded.


### Landholding

The PMAY-G eligibility questionnaire uses:

Irrigated land:
2.5 acres or more → exclusion.

Unirrigated land:
5 acres or more → exclusion.

The complete PMAY-G selection/verification framework should be used
rather than treating these questions as a standalone application
form.


# B. PMAY-U 2.0 — Urban

### Urban Residence

The family must live in an urban area falling under the applicable
PMAY-U 2.0 jurisdiction.


### Income

EWS:
Annual household income up to ₹3 lakh.

LIG:
Annual household income above ₹3 lakh and up to ₹6 lakh.

MIG:
Annual household income above ₹6 lakh and up to ₹9 lakh.


### Existing Pucca House

The family must not own a pucca house anywhere in India.

This is a major current PMAY-U 2.0 eligibility condition.


### Previous Housing Benefit

A beneficiary who has been allotted a house under a Central,
State/UT Government or Local Self Government housing scheme during
the previous 20 years is not eligible for PMAY-U 2.0.

The scheme guidelines require verification to prevent duplicate
housing benefits.


### Aadhaar

All eligible beneficiaries/family members must have Aadhaar or
Aadhaar Virtual ID integrated with beneficiary details.

If an eligible beneficiary does not have Aadhaar/Aadhaar Virtual ID,
the State/UT is required to facilitate enrolment.


### Priority Groups

PMAY-U 2.0 gives preference to:

- Widows
- Single women
- Persons with Disabilities
- Senior citizens
- Transgender persons
- Scheduled Castes
- Scheduled Tribes
- Minorities
- Other weaker/vulnerable sections

Special focus includes:

- Safai Karmis
- Street vendors identified under PMSVANidhi
- Artisans under PM Vishwakarma
- Anganwadi workers
- Building and construction workers
- Residents of slums/chawls
- Other identified vulnerable groups


## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

# PMAY-G

The PMAY-G eligibility filter excludes households satisfying
specified exclusion conditions, including:

- Owning a pucca house
- House with more than two rooms
- Owning a motorised three/four-wheeler
- Owning mechanised agricultural equipment of the specified type
- Kisan Credit Card limit ₹50,000 or above
- Government employee in household
- Registered non-agricultural enterprise
- Household member earning more than ₹15,000/month
- Income-tax payer
- Professional-tax payer
- Irrigated land of 2.5 acres or more
- Unirrigated land of 5 acres or more

Other PMAY-G selection/deprivation criteria also apply.


# PMAY-U 2.0

A family is not eligible where:

- It already owns a pucca house anywhere in India.
- It received a house under a government/local-self-government
  housing scheme during the previous 20 years.
- It otherwise fails the applicable EWS/LIG/MIG and scheme-vertical
  conditions.
- It is seeking a benefit outside the applicable PMAY-U 2.0 vertical.


### Duplicate Benefit Prevention

PMAY-G and PMAY-U 2.0 have a government-level MIS linkage intended
to prevent duplicate beneficiaries between rural and urban housing
programmes.

Do not award both programmes to the same household without checking
the applicable jurisdiction and duplication rules.


## 6. Benefits

# PMAY-G

PMAY-G provides assistance for construction of a pucca house.

The standard central assistance framework provides:

- ₹1.20 lakh in plain areas
- ₹1.30 lakh in specified hilly/difficult/IAP areas

The beneficiary may also receive convergence benefits such as:

- MGNREGS wage support
- Toilet assistance through the applicable sanitation programme
- Other convergence facilities

The exact AP implementation package may contain additional
State/GoAP support and material assistance.


# PMAY-U 2.0

PMAY-U 2.0 provides assistance according to the selected vertical.

### BLC

Beneficiary Led Construction supports eligible EWS families for
construction/enhancement of houses on eligible land.

### AHP

Affordable Housing in Partnership supports EWS housing in
public/private partnership projects.

### ARH

Affordable Rental Housing addresses rental housing needs of
eligible urban beneficiaries.

### ISS

Interest Subsidy Scheme provides interest subsidy on eligible
housing loans subject to PMAY-U 2.0 rules.


### Current AP PMAY-U 2.0 Evidence

Government of India sanction records show PMAY-U 2.0 central
assistance approved for Andhra Pradesh BLC projects during FY
2026-27.

Therefore PMAY-U 2.0 is currently operational in Andhra Pradesh.

## 8. Application / Selection Process

# PMAY-G

PMAY-G is not simply a first-come-first-served online housing
application.

The process involves:

1. Identification through prescribed rural housing/deprivation
   databases.
2. Verification of household conditions.
3. Gram Panchayat / local-level verification.
4. Preparation of eligible beneficiary list.
5. Approval under the prescribed administrative process.
6. Sanction of house.
7. Construction-stage monitoring.
8. Instalment payments linked to construction progress.
9. Geo-tagging/photographic monitoring.
10. Completion and convergence of basic amenities.


### AwaasPlus

AwaasPlus is used for identification/survey of additional eligible
rural households under the applicable PMAY-G process.

AwaasPlus 2024 provides survey and e-KYC functionality.


# PMAY-U 2.0

Urban beneficiaries are identified through:

- Urban Local Bodies
- State/UT housing authorities
- PMAY-U 2.0 MIS
- Demand surveys
- Beneficiary verification
- CSMC/project sanction process

For BLC, beneficiary and project details are submitted through the
State/ULB system and sanctioned through the prescribed PMAY-U 2.0
process.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. "Andhra Pradesh Housing / PMAY" is an umbrella category, not one
   single beneficiary rule.

2. PMAY-G and PMAY-U 2.0 must be separate eligibility engines.

3. Rural/urban jurisdiction is the first major branching condition.

4. PMAY-U 2.0 has multiple verticals with different benefit
   mechanisms.

5. PMAY-G selection is based on prescribed rural housing/deprivation
   criteria and government verification.

6. PMAY-U 2.0 uses income categories EWS/LIG/MIG.

7. PMAY-U 2.0's no-pucca-house condition applies anywhere in India.

8. Previous government housing benefit within 20 years is an
   important PMAY-U 2.0 exclusion.

9. Current central assistance does not necessarily equal the total
   value of the house received by an Andhra Pradesh beneficiary.

10. State/GoAP contributions and convergence benefits must be stored
    separately from Central assistance.

11. Historical Navaratnalu–Pedalandariki Illu should not be merged
    with PMAY-U 2.0.

12. Current project-level sanction amounts should not be interpreted
    as individual beneficiary cash benefits.

13. Final beneficiary selection remains subject to State/ULB/
    Panchayat verification and applicable government orders.
