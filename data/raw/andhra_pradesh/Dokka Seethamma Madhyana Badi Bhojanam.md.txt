# Dokka Seethamma Madhyana Badi Bhojanam

## 1. Scheme Name

**Dokka Seethamma Madhyana Badi Bhojanam**

Also referred to as:

* **Dokka Seethamma Mid-Day Meal**
* **Dokka Seethamma Madhyana Bhojanam**
* Andhra Pradesh's school mid-day meal programme under **PM POSHAN**

---

## 2. Scheme Status

**CURRENT_STATUS:** ACTIVE_CURRENT
**SOURCE_VERIFICATION_STATUS:** VERIFIED_CURRENT

Dokka Seethamma Madhyana Badi Bhojanam is the Andhra Pradesh implementation/branding of the school mid-day meal programme. Current Andhra Pradesh district government information describes the programme as providing standardized nutritious meals to government-school students. ([Ankapalli District][1])

At the Union level, the programme is implemented under **Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman (PM POSHAN)**. The official PM POSHAN system continues to record Andhra Pradesh with **43,218 schools and 25,51,044 enrolments** in its 2026 reporting data. ([PM Poshan][2])

---

## 3. Objective

The objective is to provide **nutritional support to school children** while supporting:

* School enrolment
* Attendance
* Retention
* Improved nutritional status
* Access to a hot cooked meal during the school day.

The Union Government describes PM POSHAN as a rights-based centrally sponsored scheme providing nutritional support through hot cooked meals to eligible students. ([PM Poshan][3])

---

## 4. Target Beneficiaries

The primary beneficiaries are **eligible school students in Andhra Pradesh's covered schools**.

The PM POSHAN framework covers eligible students in:

* Government schools
* Government-aided schools
* Local-body schools
* Other categories covered under the scheme guidelines.

The official PM POSHAN Andhra Pradesh data currently records more than **25 lakh student enrolments** across the State's reporting school system. ([PM Poshan][2])

---

## 5. Benefit Provided

The benefit is an **in-kind nutritional benefit**, not a cash transfer.

Students receive a **hot cooked meal during the school day**.

Andhra Pradesh's Dokka Seethamma programme focuses on improving and standardizing the meal supplied to government-school students, including regional food preferences. ([Ankapalli District][1])

The exact menu and nutritional quantities are governed by the applicable PM POSHAN/state implementation guidelines and can vary according to the applicable category and implementation year.

---

## 6. Eligibility Criteria

### A. Student Status

The beneficiary must be an **eligible student enrolled in a school covered by the PM POSHAN/state school-meal programme**.

### B. School Type

Eligibility is linked primarily to the **type of school and class covered by the programme**, rather than household income.

### C. Income

No individual family-income test is required for a student to receive the regular school meal under the programme.

Therefore, the RAG system should **not import income limits from schemes such as Thalliki Vandanam or Jnanabhumi scholarships**.

### D. Caste

There is no general caste-based eligibility condition for receiving the regular school meal.

### E. Gender

The benefit is available to eligible students regardless of gender.

---

## 7. Class / School Coverage

PM POSHAN historically focuses on children in the prescribed school classes/categories under its operational guidelines.

The exact coverage should be stored as **versioned programme rules**, because PM POSHAN's scope and approved categories are determined through Union Government guidelines and annual State AWP&B approvals.

The Andhra Pradesh PM POSHAN system currently reports **43,218 schools** and **25,51,044 enrolments**. ([PM Poshan][2])

---

## 8. Required Documents

Students do **not normally submit an individual welfare application** to receive the school meal.

Eligibility is primarily established through:

* School enrolment
* School category
* Applicable PM POSHAN coverage
* School attendance/meal records.

Therefore, the RAG system should **not require**:

* Income certificate
* Land records
* Vehicle ownership information
* Property records
* Caste certificate

unless a particular implementation rule explicitly requires such information for a separate purpose.

---

## 9. Application / Delivery Mechanism

This is a **school-based delivery programme**.

The meal is prepared and served through participating schools under the State's PM POSHAN implementation system.

The official Andhra Pradesh district education page reports:

* Schools covered
* Students covered
* Meal uptake
* Cook-cum-helpers
* Ayahs and other supporting personnel

for Dokka Seethamma Madhyana Badi Bhojanam. ([Ankapalli District][1])

Thus, a student generally does **not apply individually** in the manner used for a direct-benefit scheme.

---

## 10. Implementing Department

The programme is implemented through the:

**School Education Department, Government of Andhra Pradesh**

under the broader:

**Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman (PM POSHAN)** framework of the Government of India.

The Union Ministry of Education's PM POSHAN system maintains State-level implementation and monitoring information for Andhra Pradesh. ([PM Poshan][2])

The deterministic eligibility engine should primarily verify:

```text
student_enrolled
AND eligible_school
AND applicable_programme_category

```

The system should then determine whether the student falls within the relevant meal coverage.

The LLM should **not determine eligibility using socioeconomic assumptions**.

Andhra Pradesh district government information currently records implementation of **Dokka Seethamma Madhyana Badi Bhojanam**.

For example, Anakapalli District reports:

* **1,407 schools covered**
* **94,615 students covered**

* **2,642 cook-cum-helpers**
* **1,550 ayahs**

under the programme information displayed by the district administration. ([Ankapalli District][1])

At the national PM POSHAN level, Andhra Pradesh's 2026 system data records **43,218 schools** and **25,51,044 enrolments**. ([PM Poshan][2])

## 13. Current-Status Interpretation

The current evidence supports treating the scheme as **ACTIVE_CURRENT**.

The Union Government's PM POSHAN programme is currently being administered, with official 2026 reporting infrastructure and Andhra Pradesh State-level data available. ([PM Poshan][2])

There is one important versioning issue:

The official PM POSHAN page states that the scheme had been approved through **2025-26** and was due for appraisal for the **2026-27 to 2030-31** cycle. It also states that existing schemes ending on 31 March 2026 were temporarily extended up to **30 September 2026 or approval of the new cycle, whichever was earlier**, with the existing scope and terms during the extension. ([PM Poshan][4])

Therefore:

> The RAG record should maintain **2026-27 PM POSHAN coverage as a versioned/current-policy field**, rather than assuming that every historical PM POSHAN rule automatically applies unchanged for the entire 2026-27 academic year.

---

## 14. Important Scheme Distinction

Do not confuse Dokka Seethamma Madhyana Badi Bhojanam with:

* **Thalliki Vandanam** — financial assistance to mothers/guardians
* **Vidya Mitra School Kits** — educational materials
* **Jnanabhumi Post-Matric Scholarships** — higher-education financial support
* **NTR Vaidya Seva** — healthcare
* Other nutrition programmes such as **Anganwadi supplementary nutrition**.

Dokka Seethamma is specifically the **school meal/nutritional support programme**.
