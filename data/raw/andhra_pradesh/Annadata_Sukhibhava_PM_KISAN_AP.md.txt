# Annadata Sukhibhava – PM-KISAN

## 1. Basic Information

Scheme ID: AP_ANNADATA_SUKHIBHAVA_PM_KISAN

Official Scheme Name: Annadata Sukhibhava – PM-KISAN

State: Andhra Pradesh

Government: Government of Andhra Pradesh

Department: Department of Agriculture, Government of Andhra Pradesh

Implementing Agency:
Department of Agriculture, Government of Andhra Pradesh, in coordination with the Government of India for the PM-KISAN component.

Category: Agriculture / Farmer Welfare

Subcategory: Direct Investment / Income Support

Scheme Status: ACTIVE_CURRENT

Current As Of: September 2026

Launch / Implementation:
The current Annadata Sukhibhava programme was implemented from 2025-26.
The Andhra Pradesh Government is continuing the programme during 2026-27.

Relationship with PM-KISAN:
Annadata Sukhibhava is an Andhra Pradesh farmer-support programme implemented in conjunction with the central PM-KISAN scheme.

The total annual support described by the Andhra Pradesh Government is ₹20,000 per farmer family under the combined Annadata Sukhibhava–PM-KISAN arrangement.

The Andhra Pradesh State Government contribution is ₹14,000 per year and the PM-KISAN component is ₹6,000 per year for eligible PM-KISAN beneficiaries.

Important:
The eligibility rules of the Andhra Pradesh state component and the central PM-KISAN component must not be treated as identical.


## 2. Scheme Overview

Annadata Sukhibhava is an Andhra Pradesh Government farmer welfare programme intended to provide financial support for agricultural investment and farmer-family needs.

The Andhra Pradesh Government's budget documents state that the scheme provides financial assistance of ₹20,000 per annum to farmers.

The Andhra Pradesh Government has also explicitly described the scheme as covering farmer families including tenant farmers and Recognition of Forest Rights (ROFR) farmers.

Current Andhra Pradesh Agriculture Department material additionally describes state-budget assistance of ₹20,000 per year for landless cultivators.

The central PM-KISAN component provides ₹6,000 per year to eligible landholding farmer families under the separate PM-KISAN eligibility rules.

Therefore, the RAG should distinguish:

1. Annadata Sukhibhava – Andhra Pradesh state support.
2. PM-KISAN – Government of India support.
3. Combined Annadata Sukhibhava–PM-KISAN implementation.


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

Current Andhra Pradesh government sources identify farmer beneficiaries including:

- Farmer families owning cultivable agricultural land
- Tenant farmers
- Recognition of Forest Rights (ROFR) land cultivators / farmer families
- Landless cultivators, for the state-budget assistance described by the Agriculture Department

The Andhra Pradesh Agriculture Department describes the programme as providing ₹20,000 annual support to farmer families.

Important:

The PM-KISAN component is separately restricted to eligible landholding farmer families under PM-KISAN rules.

Therefore:

Landholding farmer family:
May be eligible for the combined Annadata Sukhibhava–PM-KISAN support subject to applicable state and PM-KISAN rules.

ROFR cultivator:
Recognized by Andhra Pradesh government sources as a beneficiary category.

Tenant farmer:
Recognized by Andhra Pradesh government budget material as a beneficiary category.

Landless cultivator:
The Andhra Pradesh Agriculture Department states that state-budget assistance is provided to landless cultivators.

Exact category-wise current implementation rules require verification against the latest Agriculture Department beneficiary-selection guidelines.

Annadata Sukhibhava state-component family rules must be interpreted according to the current Andhra Pradesh implementation guidelines.

Do not assume that every state-component beneficiary must independently satisfy all PM-KISAN family conditions.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

### Annadata Sukhibhava State Component

A universal current exclusion list could not be conclusively verified from the current Andhra Pradesh sources reviewed.

Do not automatically apply the PM-KISAN exclusion list to every Annadata Sukhibhava state beneficiary.

### PM-KISAN Component

The Government of India identifies the following major exclusion categories:

- Institutional landholders
- Former and present holders of constitutional posts
- Former and present Ministers / State Ministers
- Former and present Members of Parliament
- Former and present Members of State Legislative Assemblies / Councils
- Former and present Mayors of Municipal Corporations
- Former and present Chairpersons of District Panchayats
- Serving or retired Central/State Government officers and employees
- Employees of Central/State Public Sector Enterprises
- Employees of autonomous institutions under government
- Regular employees of local bodies
- Retired pensioners receiving ₹10,000 or more per month, subject to the stated exceptions
- Persons who paid income tax in the previous assessment year
- Practising registered professionals including doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects

PM-KISAN also has specific land-record and family-level requirements.

These exclusions apply to the PM-KISAN component and must not automatically be represented as the complete exclusion list for the Andhra Pradesh state component.


## 6. Benefits

### Total Combined Annual Support

The Andhra Pradesh Government describes total annual support of:

₹20,000 per farmer family per year

The combined amount consists of:

- ₹14,000 from the Government of Andhra Pradesh
- ₹6,000 from the Government of India through PM-KISAN

### Annadata Sukhibhava State Component

Annual state contribution:
₹14,000 per eligible beneficiary/farmer family under the applicable state rules.

### PM-KISAN Component

Annual central contribution:
₹6,000 per eligible PM-KISAN farmer family.

PM-KISAN pays the ₹6,000 in three equal instalments of ₹2,000.

### Annadata Sukhibhava Instalments

The combined programme is implemented through multiple instalments.

For the 2026-27 first instalment, the official Eluru district government release records:

- ₹5,000 from the Andhra Pradesh Government
- ₹2,000 through PM-KISAN
- Total first instalment: ₹7,000

The exact timing and amount of subsequent instalments can vary by financial year and government release schedule.

Therefore, the instalment schedule should be stored as a year-specific timeline rather than treated as a permanent rule.

## 8. Application Process

The current programme appears to be substantially based on government beneficiary identification, agriculture records and verification rather than a simple standalone public application form.

Current AP government sources identify:

- Agriculture Department implementation
- Rythu Seva Kendras as part of the agriculture service infrastructure
- Land-record modernization
- Bank-account issue resolution
- Direct transfer of assistance to farmer accounts

The PM-KISAN component has its own official registration and eKYC process.

PM-KISAN eKYC is mandatory for registered PM-KISAN farmers according to the current PM-KISAN portal.

For Annadata Sukhibhava, the exact current state-side application/update workflow must follow the latest Andhra Pradesh Agriculture Department instructions.

Do not state the following as universally verified without a current official SOP:

- Mandatory RSK application for every beneficiary
- Mandatory biometric eKYC for every Annadata beneficiary
- Mandatory MeeBhoomi cross-check for every category
- A fixed online application workflow
- A specific application deadline

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. Annadata Sukhibhava and PM-KISAN must be represented as related but distinct components.

2. The ₹20,000 figure is the combined annual support described by Andhra Pradesh Government sources.

3. The ₹6,000 PM-KISAN component remains subject to the separate central PM-KISAN eligibility and exclusion rules.

4. Andhra Pradesh Government sources explicitly mention tenant farmers and ROFR farmers.

5. The Tirupati Agriculture Department page additionally identifies state-budget assistance for landless cultivators.

6. The original draft's 5-acre ceiling was removed because it was not supported by the current authoritative AP sources reviewed.

7. The original draft's statement that every beneficiary must already be an active PM-KISAN beneficiary was removed because it is too broad and conflicts with AP sources identifying ROFR and landless cultivator beneficiaries.

8. Historical YSR Rythu Bharosa rules must not be used as current Annadata Sukhibhava rules.

9. Instalment amounts can vary by financial year and release schedule.

10. The current official Annadata portal should be checked during future maintenance/re-verification.

11. Final beneficiary determination is made by the competent Government of Andhra Pradesh / Government of India authorities according to the applicable component rules and verification records.
