## 2. Scheme Overview

Jagananna Chedodu was introduced by the Government of Andhra Pradesh
to provide financial assistance to traditional service-occupation
workers.

The scheme covers:

- Rajakas / washermen
- Nayee Brahmins / barbers
- Tailors

The original Government Order sanctioned financial assistance of
₹10,000 per year to eligible persons having shops and engaged in
their respective traditional occupations.

The objective was to provide economic assistance and support the
livelihood/self-employment activities of these workers.

The scheme was implemented through the Backward Classes Welfare
administration and district-level BC Welfare machinery.

The benefit was intended to support:

- Purchase of tools
- Equipment
- Working requirements
- Livelihood strengthening
- Continuation of traditional occupations


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

The original scheme covers:

### Rajakas

Persons engaged in washing/laundry work.

### Nayee Brahmins

Persons engaged in barber/hair-cutting services.

### Tailors

Persons engaged in tailoring work.

The official district BC Corporation description states that
Rajakas, Nayee Brahmins and Tailors belonging to all communities
were covered, subject to the applicable scheme conditions.

Therefore the occupation is a core eligibility condition.

The scheme is NOT a general-purpose financial assistance programme
for all BC households.


## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### Occupation

The applicant must belong to one of the covered traditional
occupations:

- Rajaka / washerman
- Nayee Brahmin / barber
- Tailor

The applicant should be actively engaged in the relevant occupation.


### Shop / Establishment

The original Government Order description specifies eligible
beneficiaries as persons having shops and living on the relevant
profession.

Therefore the applicant's livelihood activity/business establishment
is an important eligibility factor.

Do not assume that merely possessing the relevant caste/community
certificate automatically qualifies a person.


### Community / Caste

The official district BC Corporation description states that
Rajakas, Nayee Brahmins and Tailors belonging to all communities
are covered.

Therefore:

Community restriction:

scheme description for the three occupations.

However, occupation must be established.


### Residence

The beneficiary must be identified through the applicable Andhra
Pradesh government/local beneficiary system.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

The following should be treated as core reasons for non-eligibility:

- Applicant is not engaged in a covered occupation.
- Applicant cannot establish the relevant livelihood activity.
- Applicant does not satisfy the applicable beneficiary/database
  verification requirements.
- Duplicate beneficiary records.
- False or unsupported occupation information.

### Do Not Automatically Exclude Based On

The reviewed primary source does not establish universal current
rules for:

- Specific income ceiling
- Landholding ceiling
- Vehicle ownership
- Electricity consumption
- Property size
- Age limit
- Education level

These must not be invented or imported from another AP welfare
scheme.


## 6. Benefits

### Financial Assistance

Original/officially documented benefit:

₹10,000 per annum.

The official BC Corporation pages describe the amount as:

₹10,000 per year.

The scheme was intended to provide livelihood assistance to:

- Rajakas
- Nayee Brahmins

### Intended Use

The assistance is intended to support the beneficiary's livelihood
and traditional occupation.

Examples include:

- Tools
- Equipment
- Shop/workplace requirements
- Other occupation-related needs

The benefit should be represented as financial assistance rather
than a loan.


### Total Benefit

Important:

a current continuation order confirms it.

## 8. Application Process

Historical implementation involved beneficiary identification and
verification through the Andhra Pradesh welfare administration.

A typical workflow is:

1. Applicant is identified under the applicable household/
   beneficiary survey.
2. Occupation is verified.
3. Caste/community and identity records are checked.
4. Local-level verification is conducted.
5. Eligible/ineligible lists are prepared.
6. Social-audit/reverification procedures are conducted where
   applicable.
7. Final beneficiary list is approved.
8. Financial assistance is transferred through the government
   payment mechanism.

### Current Application Status

The National Government Services Portal currently lists separate
application-status services for:

- Financial Assistance to Tailors
- Financial Assistance to Nayee Brahmins
- Financial Assistance to Rajakas

under Jagananna Chedodu.

This confirms that application-status infrastructure remains
traceable in the government services ecosystem.

It does NOT by itself establish that a new 2026 payment cycle is
open.

## 12. Related / Linked Government Programs

### YSR Cheyutha

YSR Cheyutha was a separate livelihood-support programme for
eligible women from specified communities.

Do not merge its age/community/benefit rules with Jagananna Chedodu.


### Kapu Nestham

Kapu Nestham is a separate livelihood-support scheme for eligible
Kapu-community women.

Do not merge its rules with Chedodu.


### EBC Nestham

EBC Nestham is a separate livelihood-support programme.

Do not use its income/age rules for Chedodu.


### Jagananna Thodu

Jagananna Thodu is a separate working-capital support programme
for street vendors, small traders and traditional-artisan
businesses.

It should not be merged with Chedodu.

### BC Corporation Self-Employment Loans

BC Corporation loan/subsidy programmes are separate from Chedodu's
financial assistance.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. This scheme should not be marked simply ACTIVE_CURRENT without a
   current continuation/payment order.

2. The official AP district pages are still useful primary evidence
   for the scheme's original rules.

3. Current government service listings prove that Chedodu-related
   status infrastructure remains available, but do not prove that a
   new 2026 payment cycle is active.

4. The historical benefit is ₹10,000 per year.

5. The three occupation categories are central to eligibility.

6. Do not make caste alone sufficient for eligibility.

7. The beneficiary must be engaged in the relevant traditional
   occupation.

8. Do not import income/land/vehicle rules from another welfare
   scheme.

9. If a future AP Government Order renews Chedodu, create a new
   version:

   AP_JAGANANNA_CHEDODU_2026

10. Preserve the 2019-2024 rules as historical rather than silently
    replacing them.

11. Application-status availability should not be interpreted as
    proof of current financial sanction.

12. The RAG should answer current queries with:
    "Current 2026 continuation not verified"
    unless a newer authoritative order/payment notification is
    available.
