#YSR Nethanna Nestham

## 1. Scheme Name

**YSR Nethanna Nestham**

## 2. Scheme Status

**CURRENT_STATUS:** ACTIVE_SCHEME_DOCUMENTATION / CURRENT_2026_PAYMENT_CYCLE_NOT_CONFIRMED
**SOURCE_VERIFICATION_STATUS:** VERIFIED_SCHEME_RULES_WITH_CURRENT-CYCLE_CAVEAT

The Andhra Pradesh Department of Handlooms and Textiles continues to publish YSR Nethanna Nestham as a financial-assistance scheme for handloom weaver families. Government district pages also continue to list the scheme and its eligibility conditions. However, the latest clearly documented beneficiary/payment data located in the official sources is for **2023-24**; a fresh 2026-27 payment notification was not conclusively established. ([Tirupati District][1])

---

## 3. Objective

The objective of YSR Nethanna Nestham is to provide financial assistance to **handloom weaver families who own a handloom**, enabling them to:

* Modernize their handloom equipment
* Adopt improved handloom technologies
* Improve productivity
* Compete with the powerloom sector
* Improve their socio-economic conditions

The scheme was formally introduced through **G.O.Ms.No.89, Industries, Infrastructure, Investment and Commerce (Prog-II) Department, dated 23-10-2019**. ([Handlooms & Textiles][2])

---

## 4. Target Beneficiaries

The documented target beneficiaries are:

* **Handloom weaver families**
* Family must **own a handloom**
* Weaver must be **actually engaged in handloom weaving**
* Weaving should be the source of livelihood
* Family must belong to the **Below Poverty Line (BPL)** category. ([Tirupati District][1])

---

## 5. Benefit Provided

The documented scheme benefit is:

* **₹24,000 per year**
* Paid to an eligible **weaver family**
* One family receives **only one benefit**, irrespective of the number of handlooms owned.

The financial assistance is intended primarily for modernization of handloom equipment and improving the family's ability to compete with powerloom products. ([Handlooms & Textiles][2])

---

## 6. Eligibility Criteria

### A. Handloom Ownership

The family must **own a handloom**.

This is a core eligibility condition of the scheme. ([Handlooms & Textiles][2])

### B. Active Weaving Profession

The weaver must:

* Be engaged in handloom weaving
* Depend on weaving for livelihood.

Government district Handlooms and Textiles pages explicitly state this requirement. ([Tirupati District][1])

### C. BPL Status

The weaver family must belong to the:

**Below Poverty Line (BPL)** category. ([Handlooms & Textiles][2])

### D. One Benefit Per Family

Only **one benefit is allowed per weaver family**, irrespective of the number of handlooms owned by the family. ([Handlooms & Textiles][2])

### E. Gender

The official scheme order does **not restrict the benefit to women**.

Therefore, the RAG system should not introduce a female-only condition.

---

## 7. Family Definition

The scheme is structured around the **weaver family**, rather than providing a separate ₹24,000 payment for every individual loom or every individual family member.

The Government Order explicitly states:

> One weaver family shall be eligible for only one benefit, irrespective of the number of looms owned.

([Handlooms & Textiles][2])

---

## 8. Required Documents

The official sources reviewed establish:

* Proof of handloom ownership
* Verification of handloom weaving activity
* BPL/family eligibility verification

However, the sources reviewed do **not establish a complete current 2026 document checklist**.

Therefore, documents such as Aadhaar, bank passbook, caste certificate, income certificate, etc. should **not be hard-coded as mandatory scheme documents** unless confirmed by the relevant current implementation instructions.

## 9. Application / Delivery Mechanism

Government district Handlooms and Textiles pages identify the **Village/Ward Secretariat system** for registration.

For example, official district documentation states that applicants should be registered through the **Welfare and Education Assistant at Village/Ward Secretariats**, with the scheme linked to the GSWS/NBM system. ([Guntur District][3])

The RAG system should nevertheless distinguish this documented workflow from a **new 2026 application cycle**, which was not independently confirmed.

## 13. Current-Status Interpretation

There are two separate pieces of information that should be retained in the RAG database.

### Scheme Documentation

The official AP Handlooms and Textiles website and current district government pages continue to describe YSR Nethanna Nestham as a financial-assistance scheme for handloom weavers. ([Handlooms & Textiles][4])

### Current 2026-27 Payment Cycle

The latest clearly documented beneficiary/payment data located in these official sources is for **2023-24**.

Therefore:

> The RAG system can state that the documented scheme provides **₹24,000 per eligible weaver family per year**, but should not automatically state that a fresh ₹24,000 payment is currently being disbursed in 2026-27 without a current Government notification.

---

## 14. Important Scheme Distinction

Do not confuse YSR Nethanna Nestham with:

* Weaver pension under **NTR Bharosa**
* Weaver **MUDRA** loans
* PMJJBY
* PMSBY
* National Handloom Development Programme
* Handloom cluster-development schemes
* Other textile/handloom assistance programmes.

Nethanna Nestham is specifically the **₹24,000 annual financial-assistance scheme for eligible handloom weaver families owning handlooms**. ([Handlooms & Textiles][2])
