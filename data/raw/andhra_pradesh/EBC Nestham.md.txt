## 2. Scheme Status

**CURRENT_STATUS:** HISTORICAL / CURRENT-CONTINUATION NOT CONFIRMED
**SOURCE_VERIFICATION_STATUS:** VERIFIED_SCHEME_RULES_WITH_CURRENT-CYCLE_CAVEAT

The Government of Andhra Pradesh district BC Corporation pages currently continue to list EBC Nestham and its documented scheme details. However, the available official material reviewed does **not establish a fresh 2026-27 payment cycle or revised 2026 rules**. Therefore, the scheme should not be represented as a confirmed active 2026-27 benefit without a current Government Order or implementation notification. ([Kakinada District][1])

---

## 3. Objective

The objective of EBC Nestham is to **financially empower women belonging to Economically Backward Classes (EBC)** by providing financial assistance for their livelihood and economic development.

The scheme was introduced through **G.O.Ms.No.2, BC Welfare (C) Department, dated 20-04-2021**. ([Kakinada District][1])

---

## 4. Target Beneficiaries

The documented scheme targets:

* **EBC women**
* Age **45 years and above and below 60 years**
* EBC communities other than:

  * SC
  * ST
  * BC
  * Kapu
  * Minority communities

The AP district BC Corporation documentation explicitly describes the beneficiary group as EBC communities other than these categories. ([Kakinada District][1])

---

## 5. Benefit Provided

The documented benefit is:

* **₹15,000 per year**
* For **3 years**
* Total assistance: **₹45,000**

The benefit is intended for the livelihood and economic empowerment of eligible EBC women. ([Kakinada District][1])

---

## 6. Eligibility Criteria

### A. Community

The beneficiary must belong to an eligible **EBC community**.

The documented EBC category excludes:

* SC
* ST
* BC
* Kapu
* Minority communities. ([Kakinada District][1])

### B. Gender

The scheme is for **women beneficiaries**. ([Kakinada District][1])

### C. Age

The beneficiary must:

* Have completed **45 years**
* Be **below 60 years**

The age condition is documented with reference to the date specified for the scheme's Government Order. ([Kakinada District][1])

### D. Family Definition

The scheme identifies the family as:

* Father
* Mother
* Dependent children. ([Konaseema][2])

### E. Family Income

Total family income should be:

* **Less than ₹10,000 per month in rural areas**
* **Less than ₹12,000 per month in urban areas**. ([Kakinada District][1])

### F. Landholding

Total family landholding should be:

* Less than **3 acres wet land**, or
* Less than **10 acres dry land**, or
* Within the documented combined **10-acre** limit. ([Kakinada District][1])

### G. Government Employee / Pensioner

* No family member should be a Government employee.
* No family member should be a Government pensioner.
* **Families of sanitary employees are exempted** under the documented criteria. ([Kakinada District][1])

### H. Four-Wheeler

The family should not own a four-wheeler.

The documented exemptions include:

* Taxi
* Tractor
* Auto. ([Kakinada District][1])

### I. Income Tax

* No family member should be an **income-tax payee**. ([Kakinada District][1])

### J. Municipal Property

In municipal areas, the documented criterion is:

* No property, or
* Built-up area **less than 1,000 sq. ft.** for residential/commercial property. ([Kakinada District][1])

## 11. Historical Implementation

The official Konaseema District BC Corporation records **EBC Nestham achievements for 2021-22**.

For Konaseema district:

* **11,053 beneficiaries**
* **₹1,657.95 lakh** recorded assistance. ([Konaseema][2])

Kakinada District records for 2021-22 show:

* **7,702 beneficiaries**
* **₹1,155.3 lakh** assistance. ([Kakinada District][1])

These are historical implementation figures and should not be interpreted as 2026 beneficiary counts.

## 12. Important Scheme Distinction

EBC Nestham should be kept separate from:

* Kapu Nestham
* Jagananna Chedodu
* YSR Cheyutha
* NTR Bharosa Pension Scheme
* Post-Matric Scholarships
* Other BC/EBC welfare schemes

In particular, **EBC Nestham and Kapu Nestham have similar age and economic filters but are separate schemes with different beneficiary categories and different scheme orders**.
