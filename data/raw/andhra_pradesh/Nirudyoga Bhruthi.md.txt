# Nirudyoga Bhruthi / Yuva Galam

## 1. Scheme Name

**Nirudyoga Bhruthi / Yuva Galam**

Also referred to as:

* **Yuva Galam Nidhi**
* **Yuva Galam Fund**
* **Unemployment Allowance for Youth**
* **Nirudyoga Bhruthi**

There are two distinct records that must not be merged:

1. **Mukhyamantri Yuvanestham / Nirudyoga Bhruthi** — the earlier AP unemployment-allowance programme.
2. **Yuva Galam / Yuva Galam Nidhi** — the ₹3,000/month unemployment-allowance proposal associated with the 2024 electoral programme.

---

## 2. Scheme Status

**CURRENT_STATUS:** PROPOSED / IMPLEMENTATION NOT CONFIRMED
**SOURCE_VERIFICATION_STATUS:** VERIFIED_PROPOSAL_WITHOUT_CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDELINES

The **₹3,000/month Yuva Galam unemployment allowance** was announced as a proposed benefit for unemployed youth. Available reporting does not establish a Government of Andhra Pradesh scheme notification containing final eligibility rules, application procedure and payment commencement.

The earlier **Mukhyamantri Yuvanestham** was a separate implemented unemployment-allowance scheme under the previous administration. Government budget documentation records that its allowance was increased from ₹1,000 to **₹2,000/month** and that 4.3 lakh beneficiaries had been sanctioned. ([Amazon Web Services, Inc.][1])

Therefore, these two programmes should be **versioned separately in the RAG database**.

---

## 3. Objective

The proposed Yuva Galam unemployment allowance is intended to provide **financial support to unemployed youth until they obtain employment**, alongside the broader objective of creating employment opportunities.

The announced proposal was for:

* Employment opportunities for youth
* **₹3,000/month unemployment allowance**
* Assistance until employment is obtained. ([The New Indian Express][2])

---

## 4. Target Beneficiaries

The proposal targets:

* **Unemployed youth**
* Youth seeking employment in Andhra Pradesh

The publicly announced proposal did not establish a sufficiently detailed final beneficiary definition.

Therefore, the RAG system should **not invent**:

* Exact age range
* Educational qualification
* Income ceiling
* Employment-exchange registration requirement
* Family definition
* Number of beneficiaries per household

until official implementation guidelines are available.

---

## 5. Benefit Proposed

The publicly announced benefit is:

* **₹3,000 per month**
* Intended as an unemployment allowance
* Proposed to continue **until the beneficiary obtains employment**. ([The New Indian Express][2])

This should be stored as:

```text
PROPOSED_BENEFIT = ₹3,000/month
```

and **not**:

```text
CURRENT_DISBURSEMENT = ₹3,000/month
```

because current implementation has not been conclusively established.

---

## 6. Eligibility Criteria

### A. Employment Status

The proposal targets **unemployed youth**.

However, the precise legal definition of "unemployed" has not been established through a current Government Order.

### B. Age

A final official age range for the proposed Yuva Galam allowance was **not verified**.

Do not automatically reuse the age criteria of Mukhyamantri Yuvanestham.

### C. Education

The proposal refers to unemployed youth, but a final educational qualification was not established.

### D. Income

No final official family-income ceiling was verified.

### E. Employment Exchange / Registration

A current mandatory employment-registration condition was not verified.

### F. Other Exclusions

The following should remain **UNVERIFIED** until final guidelines are issued:

* Government employee exclusion
* Pensioner exclusion
* Income-tax payer exclusion
* Vehicle ownership
* Landholding
* Property ownership
* Electricity consumption
* Family income

## 11. Verification / Selection Conditions

The eligibility engine should **not be activated** for the proposed Yuva Galam allowance.

If a user asks:

> "Am I eligible for Yuva Galam?"

```text

```

If those rules are unavailable, the system should return:

```text

```

rather than making an eligibility determination.

---

## 12. Historical Context — Mukhyamantri Yuvanestham

The earlier **Mukhyamantri Yuvanestham** must be maintained as a separate historical scheme record.

Official AP budget documentation states that:

* The scheme provided unemployment allowance to educated unemployed youth.
* It was initially **₹1,000/month**.
* The amount was increased to **₹2,000/month**.
* **4.3 lakh beneficiaries** had been sanctioned.
* The scheme also sought to connect unemployed youth with entrepreneurship, skill-development and placement programmes. ([Amazon Web Services, Inc.][1])

This historical scheme should **not be merged** with the proposed ₹3,000 Yuva Galam allowance.

---


### Proposed Scheme

**Yuva Galam / Yuva Galam Nidhi**

```text
PROPOSED_BENEFIT:
₹3,000/month
```

The ₹3,000 allowance was publicly announced as part of the youth-employment programme. ([The New Indian Express][2])

However, the available evidence does **not establish a current 2026 payment system with final eligibility guidelines**.

### RAG interpretation

> **Do not classify ₹3,000/month Yuva Galam as an active 2026 welfare payment without a current Government notification.**

## 14. Important Scheme Distinction

Do not confuse:

| Scheme                                    | Status                                             |                 Documented Allowance |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -----------------------------------: |
| **Mukhyamantri Yuvanestham**              | Historical                                         |          ₹2,000/month after increase |
| **Nirudyoga Bhruthi**                     | Historical terminology associated with Yuvanestham | ₹2,000/month in later implementation |
| **Yuva Galam / Yuva Galam Nidhi**         | Proposed                                           |                         ₹3,000/month |
| **Employment/job recruitment programmes** | Separate programmes                                |        Not an unemployment allowance |

The earlier ₹2,000 programme and proposed ₹3,000 programme must have **separate source/version records**.
