# Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Vidya Mitra School Kits

## 1. Scheme Name

**Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Vidya Mitra Kit**

Also referred to as:

* **Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Vidya Mitra**
* **Vidya Mitra School Kits**
* **Vidya Mitra Kit**

---

## 2. Scheme Status

**CURRENT_STATUS:** ACTIVE_CURRENT
**SOURCE_VERIFICATION_STATUS:** VERIFIED_CURRENT

The Andhra Pradesh Department of School Education continues to list **Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Vidya Mitra (School Kits Distribution)** as a current school-education initiative. The Tirupati District Government page lists it among the department's current key schemes. ([Tirupati District][1])

The Markapuram District Government page specifically records that Vidya Mitra kits were provided to **all boys and girls during the 2025-26 academic year**. ([Markapuram][2])

---

## 3. Objective

The objective of the scheme is to provide students with essential educational materials so that they can:

* Attend school with the required learning materials
* Reduce the financial burden on families
* Access textbooks, notebooks and school-use materials
* Participate in school education without material-related barriers.

The Government of Andhra Pradesh describes the kit as a comprehensive learning kit intended to ensure that students can learn without difficulties. ([Markapuram][2])

---

## 4. Target Beneficiaries

The documented beneficiaries are **students studying in Government Schools of Andhra Pradesh**.

The AP School Education Department identifies Vidya Mitra as a school-kit distribution initiative. ([Tirupati District][1])

The Markapuram District Government page states that the kits were provided to **all boys and girls** during the 2025-26 academic year. ([Markapuram][2])

---

## 5. Benefit Provided

The scheme provides an **in-kind educational kit**, rather than a direct cash transfer.

The 2025-26 kit documented by the Markapuram District Government included:

* **1 school bag**
* **Textbooks**
* **Notebooks**
* **1 dictionary**
* **3 sets of uniforms**
* **1 pair of shoes**
* **2 pairs of socks**
* **1 belt**

([Markapuram][2])

The West Godavari District Government page also records distribution of:

* Textbooks
* Notebooks
* Uniforms
* Shoes
* Tie
* Belt
* School bags
* Dictionaries

with separate quantities for different classes. ([West Godavari District][3])

---

## 6. Eligibility Criteria

### A. Student Status

The beneficiary must be a **student enrolled in an eligible Government School**.

The scheme is delivered through the School Education Department rather than through an individual income-based application.

### B. Gender

The scheme is available to:

* Boys
* Girls

The Markapuram District Government explicitly states that the kits were supplied to **all boys and girls** for 2025-26. ([Markapuram][2])

### C. Income

No separate family-income ceiling was identified in the official sources reviewed for Vidya Mitra kits.

Therefore, the RAG system should **not import income criteria from schemes such as Thalliki Vandanam or Jnanabhumi**.

### D. Caste

No caste-based eligibility restriction was identified in the official Vidya Mitra sources reviewed.

### E. Age

No independent age criterion was identified.

Eligibility is primarily linked to **student enrolment in the covered school system**.

---

## 7. Class / School Coverage

Official district documentation records kit components for students across school classes.

West Godavari Government documentation records:

* Textbooks for **Classes 1–10**
* Notebooks for different class groups
* Uniforms for **Classes 1–10**
* School bags for **Classes 1–10**
* Shoes and socks for **Classes 1–10**
* Additional textbook/notebook/bag provisions for **Classes 11–12**. ([West Godavari District][3])

However, the exact class coverage and kit composition can vary by academic year and government procurement instructions.

Therefore, the RAG record should treat **academic-year-specific kit composition as versioned data**.

---

## 8. Required Documents

No individual beneficiary application document checklist was established from the official sources reviewed.

Because the scheme is school-based, beneficiary identification is associated with **student enrolment and school records**.

The RAG system should **not require Aadhaar, caste certificate, income certificate, land records or vehicle information** for Vidya Mitra unless a specific implementation notification establishes such a requirement.

---

## 9. Application / Delivery Mechanism

Vidya Mitra is primarily a **school-level distribution scheme**, rather than an individual cash-benefit application scheme.

The School Education Department coordinates procurement and distribution of educational materials through the school system.

The West Godavari Government page documents the distribution quantities for various kit components and classes, demonstrating the school-level supply mechanism. ([West Godavari District][3])

Therefore:

> A student should not be treated as needing to submit an individual welfare application in the same way as a cash-transfer scheme.

## 13. Current-Status Interpretation

Unlike cash-transfer schemes, Vidya Mitra is an **in-kind educational support programme**.

The official AP School Education Department continues to list it as a current initiative. ([Tirupati District][1])

The 2025-26 implementation is explicitly documented by the Markapuram District Government. ([Markapuram][2])

Therefore:

> **ACTIVE_CURRENT** is appropriate for the scheme record.

However, the **exact kit contents should be stored by academic year**, because procurement and distribution components can change.

For example, the 2025-26 documented kit contains a dictionary, three uniform sets, shoes, socks and belt, while other district documentation records additional class-specific components. ([Markapuram][2])

---

## 14. Important Scheme Distinction

Do not confuse Vidya Mitra with:

* **Thalliki Vandanam** — direct financial assistance to mothers/guardians
* **Jnanabhumi Post-Matric Scholarships** — post-matric financial support
* **Dokka Seethamma Madhyana Badi Bhojanam** — school meal programme
* **Mana Badi Mana Bhavishyathu** — school infrastructure programme
* **PM POSHAN** — centrally supported school meal programme.

Vidya Mitra is specifically an **educational-material/school-kit distribution programme**.
