## 2. Scheme Overview

Thalliki Vandanam is an Andhra Pradesh Government educational financial-assistance programme intended to support children continuing education from Class I through Class XII, including Intermediate Education.

The scheme provides financial assistance to an eligible mother or recognised guardian for each eligible child.

The scheme applies to eligible students studying in:

- Government schools
- Private aided schools
- Private unaided schools
- Recognised residential schools
- Recognised junior colleges / residential junior colleges

The scheme was implemented from academic year 2025-26 and was continued for academic year 2026-27 through G.O.Ms.No.15 dated 16.07.2026.

The benefit is ₹15,000 per eligible child per academic year.

An amount of ₹2,000 per student is deducted at source for the educational ecosystem, including school/junior-college maintenance, hygiene and sanitation and other purposes prescribed under the applicable SOP.

Therefore, the normal amount credited to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-seeded bank account is ₹13,000, subject to special payment arrangements such as RTE Section 12(1)(c) and scholarship convergence.


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

Primary beneficiary:

- Eligible mother of a child studying in Classes I-XII / Intermediate.

Recognised guardian:

- Where the mother is unavailable or where the applicable rules recognise another guardian, the benefit may be paid to the recognised guardian subject to the scheme's verification process.

Student coverage:

- Class I
- Classes II-XII
- Intermediate / Junior Intermediate
- Recognised residential school/junior-college students
- Eligible students in Government schools
- Eligible students in Private Aided schools
- Eligible students in Private Unaided schools

Special categories:

- Single-parent families
- Orphan children
- Street children enrolled through eligible voluntary organisations
- Children admitted under Section 12(1)(c) of the Right to Education Act, 2009
- Students covered under certain Centrally Sponsored Scholarship Schemes, subject to the scheme's balance-payment rules

The benefit is calculated per eligible child rather than once per family.


## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### Age

No separate beneficiary age limit for the mother/guardian is specified in the verified scheme guidelines.

Eligibility is primarily linked to the student's class, enrolment and household eligibility.

Student education level:

- Class I to Class XII
- Intermediate / Junior Intermediate

Age:
Not specified as a standalone eligibility criterion in the verified current scheme order.


### Gender

The ordinary beneficiary is the mother of the eligible child.

However, the scheme also recognises guardians and provides special provisions for:

- Single-parent families
- Cases where the mother is unavailable
- Orphan children

Therefore, the benefit is not implemented as a strict female-only payment rule in every circumstance.

Payment recipient is determined according to the applicable mother/guardian rules.


### Residence

The beneficiary should be included in the relevant Andhra Pradesh household database.

The 2026 guidelines provide for field verification where the beneficiary is not present in the household database but the child is present.

The student must be studying in a recognised institution covered by the Andhra Pradesh scheme.

State:
Andhra Pradesh.


### Income

The total household income must not exceed:

Rural:
₹10,000 per month

Urban:
₹12,000 per month

The household income condition is a household-level eligibility parameter.


### Occupation

There is no general occupation requirement for the mother/guardian.

However, employment status of household members is used as an exclusion criterion.

Government/PSU employment may disqualify a household, subject to the exemptions specified in the scheme guidelines.

Specified exemptions include:

- Sanitation workers
- Anganwadi workers
- Employees whose monthly income is below the prescribed household-income threshold, according to the applicable guideline.


### Education

Student must be studying in:

- Class I-XII, or
- Intermediate / Junior Intermediate

Institution must be recognised by the Government of Andhra Pradesh.

Students pursuing:

- ITI
- Polytechnic
- RGUKT / IIIT
- Other similar courses already benefiting from fee reimbursement

are not eligible under the specified exclusion in the Thalliki Vandanam guidelines.


### Land

The household landholding must satisfy the following:

- Less than 3 acres of wet land, OR
- Less than 10 acres of dry land, OR
- Less than 10 acres when wet and dry land are considered together.

Landholding is assessed at the household level.

Land information is subject to government-record verification.


### Social Category

The scheme is not restricted to one caste or social category.

Students/beneficiaries from SC, ST, BC, Minority, EWS and other eligible households may be covered subject to the general scheme conditions.

The 2026 guidelines specifically address students who are also covered under Centrally Sponsored Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarship schemes.

For such students, scholarship amounts are handled according to the applicable scholarship rules, with the balance payable under Thalliki Vandanam released where applicable.


### Family Requirement

The household must satisfy the scheme's household eligibility conditions.

At least one person in the household must possess a Rice Card.

The beneficiary should be present in the state household database.

Where the mother/guardian is absent from the household database but the child is present, the SGSW Department may conduct field verification and map the eligible mother/guardian according to the applicable rules.


### Attendance

For the 2025-26 implementation, the Andhra Pradesh Government's budget documentation describes the scheme as supporting mothers whose children are studying in Classes I-Intermediate and having 75% attendance.

For the 2026-27 scheme, the governing order and implementation framework continue the scheme while using the verified beneficiary/student database and eligibility screening.

Current attendance interpretation:
The exact academic-year attendance cutoff and how attendance is applied to 2026-27 beneficiaries should follow the current School Education Department implementation/SOP.

Do not assume that the 2025-26 payment rule automatically applies identically to every 2026-27 case without checking the current implementation instruction.


### Other Conditions

The household must satisfy the applicable verification parameters including:

- Rice Card condition
- Landholding condition
- Four-wheeler condition
- Electricity consumption condition
- Municipal property condition
- Government employment/pension condition
- Income-tax condition
- Household database condition

The student must be enrolled in a recognised educational institution covered by the scheme.


## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

A household is not eligible when it fails the prescribed household verification parameters.

### Income

Household income:

- More than ₹10,000/month in rural areas
- More than ₹12,000/month in urban areas


### Rice Card

At least one household member must possess a Rice Card.

Households without the required Rice Card condition are not eligible unless covered by a specific exception under the applicable government rules.


### Land

Household landholding at or above the prescribed thresholds is excluded:

- 3 acres or more wet land
- 10 acres or more dry land
- 10 acres or more combined wet/dry land

The exact treatment of mixed wet/dry holdings should follow the government's validation calculation.


### Four-Wheeler

Households owning a four-wheeler are excluded.

Exemptions specified by the guidelines include:

- Taxis
- Tractors
- Autos

where they fall under the stated livelihood exemptions.


### Electricity

Average household electricity consumption must be:

Less than 300 units per month.

The guidelines specify that the average electricity consumption over the preceding 12 months is considered.

This applies to the household's own or rented residence as specified in the guidelines.


### Municipal Property

The household should not own municipal property measuring:

1,000 square feet or more.

The current guideline uses the municipal-property condition as a household eligibility parameter.


### Government Employment / Pension

Households are excluded where a member is:

- Employed in Central Government service
- Employed in State Government service
- Employed in a PSU
- Receiving government pension after retirement

Specified exemptions include:

- Sanitation workers
- Anganwadi workers
- Employees whose salary falls below the applicable ₹10,000 rural / ₹12,000 urban monthly threshold, as specified by the guideline.


### Income Tax

If any household member is an income-tax payer, the household is not eligible.


### Student Course

Students pursuing:

- ITI
- Polytechnic
- RGUKT / IIIT
- Similar courses already receiving fee reimbursement

are excluded according to the scheme guidelines.


### Household Database

The beneficiary should be present in the Andhra Pradesh household database.

If the child is present but the mother/guardian is absent from the household database, field verification may be conducted before eligibility is established.


## 6. Benefits

### Financial Assistance

₹15,000 per eligible child per academic year.

The benefit is:

- Per child
- Not limited to one child per family

### Deduction at Source

₹2,000 per student is deducted from the sanctioned ₹15,000.

The deducted amount is used for:

- School maintenance
- Junior college maintenance
- Hygiene
- Sanitation
- Other educational ecosystem purposes prescribed by the Government.

### Normal DBT Amount

₹13,000 is normally credited to the eligible mother's/guardian's Aadhaar-seeded bank account after the ₹2,000 deduction.

### Special Payment Cases

For students admitted under Section 12(1)(c) of the Right to Education Act:

- The applicable Government-prescribed fee may be paid to the private school management through the State Project Director, Samagra Shiksha.
- The remaining eligible amount, if any, is credited to the beneficiary.

For students covered by applicable Centrally Sponsored Pre-Matric/Post-Matric Scholarship schemes:

- The admissible scholarship amount is met from the relevant Welfare Department provisions.
- The balance amount, if any, is released under Thalliki Vandanam.

### Orphan Children

Where an eligible orphan child does not have a bank account, the District Collector may facilitate a guardian-operated minor savings account according to applicable banking regulations.

The scheme amount is then disbursed according to the special orphan-child procedure.

## 8. Application Process

Thalliki Vandanam is primarily implemented through government data collection, verification, beneficiary identification and DBT rather than a conventional open application form for every student.

### Step 1 — Student Data Collection

School/educational institution data is collected from the relevant education databases.

Student information includes:

- Name
- Aadhaar
- Class
- School
- Date of birth
- Other relevant educational information


### Step 2 — Mother/Guardian Mapping

Mother/guardian information is collected and authenticated.

The system establishes the relationship between:

Student ↔ Mother/Guardian


### Step 3 — Data Validation

The SGSW Department carries out data verification including:

- Aadhaar validation
- Duplicate-record removal
- Invalid-record correction
- Mother-child mapping
- Household Survey cross-verification
- Civil Supplies/Rice Card cross-verification


### Step 4 — Eligibility Screening

The household is checked against:

- Income
- Rice Card
- Land
- Vehicle
- Electricity
- Municipal property
- Government employment/pension
- Income-tax
- Household database
- Student educational status


### Step 5 — Eligible / Ineligible Lists

Eligible and ineligible lists are displayed at the designated Swarna Gramam / Swarna Wardu offices for social audit.

### Step 6 — Grievances

Ineligible mothers/guardians can submit grievances through the designated SGSW/local administrative mechanism.

### Step 7 — Supplementary Verification

Valid grievances and corrected records are verified.

Supplementary eligible lists are prepared.

### Step 8 — Payment

The eligible amount is processed through the government payment architecture.

Normal payment:
Mother's Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

Special cases:

- Single-parent family: father's Aadhaar-seeded bank account where applicable.
- Mother unavailable: recognised guardian according to the applicable rules.
- Orphan: District Collector / guardian-operated account mechanism.
- RTE Section 12(1)(c): applicable amount routed for school reimbursement as prescribed.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. Thalliki Vandanam is a current Andhra Pradesh education-support programme.

2. The current governing guideline is G.O.Ms.No.15 dated 16.07.2026.

3. The scheme should be represented as a current programme separately from historical Amma Vodi material.

4. ₹15,000 is the sanctioned annual amount per eligible child.

5. ₹2,000 is deducted at source for educational ecosystem purposes under the current guidelines.

6. Therefore, the ordinary DBT amount to the mother's/guardian's account is ₹13,000.

7. The benefit is per eligible child, not limited to one child per family.

8. Household eligibility is evaluated using multiple government data sources.

9. Final eligibility is determined through government verification.

10. The RAG system should not treat the scheme as a simple "₹15,000 payment" without considering household, student and institution conditions.

11. Current year-specific payment schedules should be stored separately from permanent scheme rules.

12. Historical Amma Vodi information should not be used to override the current Thalliki Vandanam rules.

13. Final eligibility and payment are determined by the competent Government of Andhra Pradesh authorities.
