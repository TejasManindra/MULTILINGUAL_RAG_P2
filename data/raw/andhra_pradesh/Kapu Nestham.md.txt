## 3. Objective

The objective of Kapu Nestham is to **financially empower women belonging to Kapu, Balija, Telaga and Ontari communities** by providing livelihood-oriented financial assistance and improving their living standards.

The scheme was established under **G.O.Ms.No.4, BC Welfare (C) Department, dated 28-01-2020**. ([Kakinada District][1])

---

## 4. Target Beneficiaries

The scheme targets:

* Women belonging to the **Kapu community**
* **Balija**
* **Telaga**
* **Ontari/Vontari**
* Age group: **45 years and above and below 60 years**
* Permanent residents of Andhra Pradesh
* Economically eligible families meeting the prescribed exclusion conditions. ([Grama Awards Achivalayam][2])

---

## 5. Benefit Provided

The original scheme provides:

* **₹15,000 per year**
* For a period of **5 years**
* Total assistance: **₹75,000**
* Intended for livelihood and economic empowerment activities. ([Kakinada District][1])

The AP Government's 2023-24 budget documentation also recorded ₹15,000 annual assistance for eligible women in the 45–60 age group. ([Scribd][3])

---

## 6. Eligibility Criteria

A beneficiary must satisfy the applicable scheme conditions.

### A. Citizenship / Residence

* Must be a **permanent resident of Andhra Pradesh**. ([Grama Awards Achivalayam][2])

### B. Community

Must belong to:

* Kapu
* Balija
* Telaga
* Ontari

community. ([Grama Awards Achivalayam][2])

### C. Age

* **45 years or above**
* **Below 60 years**

The scheme documentation specifies that the age condition is assessed according to the prescribed scheme date/order. ([Kakinada District][1])

### D. Family Income

Total family income should be:

* **Less than ₹10,000/month in rural areas**
* **Less than ₹12,000/month in urban areas**. ([Kakinada District][1])

### E. Landholding

Total family landholding should be:

* Less than **3 acres wet land**, or
* Less than **10 acres dry land**, or
* Within the prescribed **10-acre combined limit**. ([Kakinada District][1])

### F. Government Employee / Pensioner

* No family member should be a Government employee or Government pensioner.
* **Sanitary workers' families are exempted** under the documented criteria. ([Kakinada District][1])

### G. Four-Wheeler

Family should not own a four-wheeler.

The documented exemptions include:

* Taxi
* Tractor
* Auto

([Grama Awards Achivalayam][2])

### H. Income Tax

* No family member should be an **income-tax payee**. ([Kakinada District][1])

### I. Municipal Property

For municipal areas:

* Built-up property should be **less than 750 sq. ft.**

The current district BC Corporation page gives this 750 sq. ft. criterion. ([Kakinada District][1])

### J. Electricity Consumption

The scheme SOP specifies:

* Monthly electricity consumption of the family dwelling unit should be **less than 300 units**. ([Grama Awards Achivalayam][2])

## 12. Historical Implementation

The scheme was formally introduced through **G.O.Ms.No.4, BC Welfare (C), dated 28-01-2020**.

Government district records document substantial implementation during the earlier scheme period. For example, Konaseema district recorded:

* **34,652 beneficiaries in 2020-21**
* **32,188 beneficiaries in 2021-22**

with corresponding financial assistance recorded by the district administration. ([Konaseema][4])

The 2023-24 AP Budget also recorded continued annual assistance of ₹15,000 for eligible women and an allocation of ₹550 crore for YSR Kapu Nestham. ([Scribd][3])

## 14. Important Distinction

Do not confuse **Kapu Nestham** with:

* EBC Nestham
* Jagananna Chedodu
* YSR Cheyutha
* Jagananna Vidya Deevena
* Jagananna Vasathi Deevena
* YSR Nethanna Nestham
* Kapu community education or welfare schemes

These are separate schemes with different beneficiary definitions and eligibility rules.
