## 2. Scheme Overview

YSR Bima is an Andhra Pradesh Government social-security scheme
intended to provide financial relief to eligible families following
the death or disability of a primary bread earner.

The scheme was introduced after the Government of India withdrew
from the earlier converged insurance arrangement from 01 April 2020.

The Government of Andhra Pradesh subsequently modified and continued
the programme as YSR Bima.

The official Andhra Pradesh Bima portal states that 100% of the
premium for the YSR Bima scheme is borne by the State Government.

The primary objectives are:

- Provide financial relief after accidental death.
- Provide relief after total or qualifying disability.
- Provide relief after natural death where applicable.
- Reduce the financial shock caused by loss of the primary bread
  earner.
- Provide associated scholarship support where applicable.


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

The current YSR Bima scheme is designed around:

- Primary bread earners
- Eligible BPL families
- Eligible beneficiaries identified through the Andhra Pradesh
  government beneficiary/database system

The official YSR Bima portal states that primary bread earners
belonging to BPL families in Andhra Pradesh are eligible.

The 2020 Government Order defines a YSR Bima beneficiary as the
primary bread earner of a BPL family.


## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### Primary Bread Earner

The beneficiary is the primary bread earner of the eligible family.

The 2020 Government Order specifically defines the YSR Bima
beneficiary in this manner.


### BPL Status

The scheme applies to eligible BPL families.

Government beneficiary databases/Rice Card information are used for
identification under the applicable implementation process.


### Age

The current official YSR Bima scheme page states:

18 to 70 years.

Therefore:

Minimum age:
18 years

Maximum age:
70 years


### Occupation

The official YSR Bima portal describes the programme in the context
of eligible unorganised workers.

The older unorganised-worker framework explicitly covered workers
registered under the applicable unorganised-worker/social-security
system.

Do not assume that every person earning an income automatically
qualifies.


### Income / Wage

Historical/current Bima portal material states that eligible
unorganised workers may have monthly wages of ₹15,000 or less.

However, because the current YSR Bima framework is based on the
modified BPL-family scheme, this should be stored with source/version
metadata rather than treated as an independently sufficient
eligibility test.

Recommended RAG field:

monthly_wage_limit:
₹15,000 or less in the applicable unorganised-worker eligibility
material.

Primary current eligibility:
BPL / eligible government beneficiary status.


### Residence

The scheme extends throughout Andhra Pradesh.

Beneficiary must belong to the Andhra Pradesh eligible beneficiary
system.


### Gender

No general gender restriction is specified for the primary
bread-earner beneficiary.

Both male and female primary bread earners may be covered where the
scheme conditions are satisfied.


### Education

No education requirement for the insured primary bread earner.


### Land

No landholding condition was identified as a universal YSR Bima
eligibility requirement.


### Social Category

No universal caste-specific eligibility condition identified.

Eligibility is primarily linked to BPL/beneficiary status and the
applicable worker/family criteria.


## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

Potential non-eligibility conditions include:

- Person outside the applicable age range.
- Person who is not an eligible beneficiary/primary bread earner.
- Family not satisfying the applicable BPL/beneficiary criteria.
- Person outside the applicable unorganised-worker framework where
  that condition is required.
- Claims that do not satisfy the applicable insurance/event rules.

### Important

Do not automatically apply Chandranna Bima's historical rules to the
current YSR Bima scheme.

Do not treat every historical PMJJBY/PMSBY/AABY rule as a current
YSR Bima rule.

The insurance component and age-specific benefit must be evaluated
against the applicable scheme version.

#### Natural Death

Therefore:

Natural death, age 51–70:

### Historical / Earlier YSR Bima Material

Another official Labour Department page describes an earlier
converged structure in which:

- 18–59:
  ₹5 lakh accidental death/total disability
  and up to ₹3.625 lakh partial disability
- 60–70:
  ₹4.25 lakh accidental death/total disability
  and up to ₹3.25 lakh partial disability
- Natural death:
  ₹30,000 under AABY
- Scholarship:
  ₹1,200 per child for up to two children

These figures belong to a different/versioned scheme structure and
must not overwrite the current YSR Bima portal's benefit values.


### Scholarship

The official Bima material identifies scholarship assistance for
children of eligible beneficiaries.

Historical structure:
₹1,200 per year per child,
up to two children,
for children studying in:

- 9th class
- 10th class
- Intermediate
- ITI

Current scholarship eligibility should be verified against the
applicable current Bima rules before being used as a deterministic
2026 rule.


## 7. Required Documents / Data

Typical claim/beneficiary processing requires information such as:

- Beneficiary identification
- BPL / Rice Card information
- Aadhaar/beneficiary database information
- Death certificate for death claims
- FIR / police documentation for qualifying accidental deaths
- Inquest report where applicable
- Medical/disability documentation for disability claims
- Bank account information
- Nominee/family information

The exact documents depend on the type of claim.

### Accidental Death

May require:

- Death certificate
- FIR
- Inquest / post-mortem documentation where applicable
- Other police/medical evidence required by the claim process

### Disability

Requires appropriate medical/disability certification according to
the applicable scheme/insurance rules.


## 8. Application / Claim Process

YSR Bima is primarily an enrolment-and-claim based social-security
scheme.

### Beneficiary Enrolment

Eligible beneficiaries are identified/registered through the
government's beneficiary and worker-registration systems.

The Labour Department, SERP and associated local administration
participate in implementation.

### Death Claim

Typical process:

1. Death is reported.
2. Death certificate is obtained.
3. Where accidental death is involved, police documents are obtained.
4. Claim documents are uploaded/processed through the Bima system.
5. Insurance/claim authority verifies the claim.
6. Eligible benefit is released to the nominee/family.


### Disability Claim

1. Disability event is reported.
2. Medical/disability documentation is obtained.
3. Claim is registered.
4. Documents are verified.
5. Applicable benefit is sanctioned.
6. Amount is released to the eligible beneficiary.


### Funeral Assistance

Historical/current implementation material refers to funeral-expense
support in applicable claim processing.

The exact amount and payment timeline should be versioned and should
not be hard-coded without the applicable current order.

## 10. Selection / Verification / Approval

Eligibility is determined through:

- Government beneficiary database
- BPL/Rice Card information
- Applicable worker registration
- Age

### Claim Verification

Claims are verified through multiple agencies.

The official Bima monitoring system shows involvement from:

- Panchayat Raj Department
- Municipal Administration
- Police Department
- Life Insurance Corporation of India
- Oriental Insurance Company
- SERP

### Accident Claims

Police documentation such as FIR and inquest records is integrated
into the claim-verification process.

### Insurance Processing

After required documentation is uploaded and verified, the claim is
processed by the applicable insurance/administrative authority.

## 12. Related / Historical Programs

### Chandranna Bima

Historical predecessor/version of the Andhra Pradesh unorganised
worker insurance programme.

The scheme was associated with convergence of:

- PMJJBY
- PMSBY
- AABY
- State accident/death/disability insurance

Do not use Chandranna Bima benefit values as automatic current
YSR Bima values.


### PMJJBY

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana is a Central Government
insurance scheme.

It was part of earlier convergence arrangements.

Current PMJJBY eligibility and benefits are governed separately by
the Government of India.


### PMSBY

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana is a Central Government
accident-insurance scheme.

It was part of earlier YSR Bima/Chandranna Bima convergence
structures.

Do not merge current PMSBY eligibility with current YSR Bima
eligibility.


### AABY

Aam Aadmi Bima Yojana formed part of earlier converged insurance
structures.

Its historical benefit amounts should not be represented as the
current YSR Bima benefit unless a current government source confirms
the continuation.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. YSR Bima and Chandranna Bima must be represented as
   versioned scheme identities.

2. The current official portal is the preferred source for current
   YSR Bima benefit values.

3. Historical district pages frequently still use "Chandranna Bima"
   terminology.

4. Do not combine the historical Chandranna Bima table with the
   current YSR Bima table.

5. PMJJBY, PMSBY and AABY were part of earlier convergence
   arrangements.

6. Current Central Government insurance rules must not automatically
   be treated as current YSR Bima rules.

7. The scheme is intended to protect the primary bread earner/family,
   not to provide a universal insurance benefit to every resident.

8. Claim eligibility depends on the cause of death/disability and
   applicable insurance rules.

9. The current official portal displays age-specific benefits.

10. The exact claim-document list should be treated as
    event-specific.

11. Death claims and disability claims have different documentary
    requirements.

12. Current claim-processing statistics on the Bima portal demonstrate
    ongoing administrative activity but should not be interpreted as
    eligibility rules.
