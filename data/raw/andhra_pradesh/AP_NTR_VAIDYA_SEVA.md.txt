## 2. Scheme Overview

Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva is a state-sponsored healthcare scheme of the
Government of Andhra Pradesh intended to provide financial protection
and cashless healthcare to eligible poor/BPL families.

The scheme provides:

- Cashless medical treatment
- Secondary and tertiary healthcare
- Treatment for specified diseases/procedures
- Services through empanelled network hospitals
- Government hospitals and eligible private/corporate hospitals
- Pre-treatment evaluation where applicable
- Hospitalisation and treatment packages
- Post-discharge follow-up services where applicable

The scheme is administered through the Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva Trust.

The scheme is designed to reduce catastrophic medical expenditure
for eligible poor families.

Eligible patients can receive treatment through empanelled network
hospitals.

Help desks staffed by Vaidya Mithras are provided at relevant
healthcare facilities to assist beneficiaries.


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

Primary target group:

Members of eligible Below Poverty Line (BPL) families identified
through the Andhra Pradesh government beneficiary database.

The official H&MFW description identifies beneficiaries as members
of BPL families:

- Enumerated in the Civil Supplies database
- Associated with a White Ration Card
- Photographed/identified in the beneficiary database
- Linked with Aadhaar according to the applicable database system

The scheme is intended to provide healthcare protection to poor
families in both rural and urban areas.

### Important

Do not interpret the scheme as automatically covering every resident
of Andhra Pradesh.

Eligibility depends on the beneficiary database and applicable
government rules.


## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### BPL / White Ration Card

The official Andhra Pradesh Health Department describes beneficiaries
as members of BPL families enumerated and photographed in the White
Ration Card/Civil Supplies database.

Therefore:

BPL / eligible White Ration Card household:
REQUIRED according to the verified scheme description.


### Aadhaar

The official scheme description identifies White Ration Card
beneficiaries linked with Aadhaar.

Aadhaar/database linkage is therefore part of beneficiary
identification and authentication.

Do not represent Aadhaar as an independent income-eligibility test.


### Residence

The scheme is an Andhra Pradesh Government programme for eligible
beneficiaries in the Andhra Pradesh beneficiary database.

No separate numerical residence-duration requirement was identified
in the current verified material.


### Income

The scheme uses BPL/eligible beneficiary identification.

A current universal numerical household-income ceiling was not
established from the authoritative current sources reviewed.

Therefore:

Income limit:
NOT_SPECIFIED AS A CURRENT NUMERICAL THRESHOLD.

The RAG should use verified beneficiary-database status rather than
inventing a monthly or annual income cutoff.


### Age

No universal age threshold was identified for the general scheme
beneficiary.

The scheme covers eligible family members rather than being an
age-based pension or scholarship.


### Gender

No general gender restriction is specified.

Eligible members of qualifying families may receive treatment,
subject to the applicable scheme and treatment rules.


### Occupation

No universal occupation requirement was identified.


### Education

No education requirement.


### Land

No universal landholding requirement identified in the verified
current scheme description.


### Social Category

No universal caste-specific eligibility rule identified in the
verified current scheme description.

Eligibility is primarily based on the government beneficiary/BPL
database.


### Family Requirement

The scheme operates on a family basis.

Financial coverage is described as a family-level floater in current
district government material.

The exact family composition is determined by the applicable
government beneficiary database.


## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

The following should be treated as non-eligibility conditions only
where supported by the current beneficiary database/rules:

- Person/family not present in the eligible beneficiary database
- Family not satisfying the applicable BPL/Rice/White Card criteria
- Treatment not included in the approved scheme package
- Treatment at a hospital that is not authorised/empanelled for the
  relevant service
- Services outside the scheme's approved benefit package

### Important

Do not hard-code the following as universal current exclusions unless
a current Government Order explicitly establishes them:

- A specific monthly income ceiling
- A specific annual income ceiling
- A specific land ceiling
- A specific vehicle restriction
- A specific electricity-consumption restriction
- A specific property-size restriction

These criteria appear in other Andhra Pradesh welfare schemes and
must not automatically be imported into NTR Vaidya Seva.


## 6. Benefits

### Primary Benefit

Cashless healthcare for eligible beneficiaries for approved
secondary and tertiary treatment.

### Treatment Coverage

The scheme covers identified diseases and approved treatment
packages/procedures.

The current district government page describes:

- 3,257 listed therapies
- 31 disease categories
- Cashless treatment

However, because other official H&MFW material currently exposes
older figures, the exact procedure count should be treated as
versioned/current-source data rather than a timeless scheme constant.


### Financial Coverage

A current district government page states:

Maximum coverage:
₹5,00,000 per family per annum on a floater basis.

Co-payment:
No co-payment under the described scheme.

IMPORTANT:
The H&MFW state-level page still contains an older ₹2.50 lakh
coverage description.

Therefore:

CURRENT_FINANCIAL_COVERAGE:
₹5 lakh according to the newer current district government
description.

LEGACY_FINANCIAL_COVERAGE:
₹2.50 lakh according to the older H&MFW page.

The RAG should retain source/date/version metadata and should not
silently merge the two.


### Cashless Treatment

Eligible treatment is provided on a cashless basis at authorised
network hospitals.

The package covers the applicable treatment period and specified
post-discharge services according to the approved package.


### Outpatient / Screening

The scheme framework includes:

- Health camps
- Screening
- Counselling
- Free OP evaluation for listed therapies where applicable

### Inpatient Treatment

For approved listed therapies, the scheme provides end-to-end
cashless treatment through an authorised network hospital.

### Post-Discharge

The scheme provides specified post-discharge medication and
follow-up services according to the applicable treatment package.

The exact number of days and follow-up period should be treated as
package-specific rather than assumed universally.


## 7. Required Documents

Common beneficiary-identification information includes:

- White Ration Card / eligible beneficiary record
- Aadhaar-linked beneficiary information
- Medical records
- Diagnostic reports where applicable
- Referral/medical documents where required

At the hospital, the beneficiary's eligibility is authenticated
through the applicable Vaidya Seva system.

### Emergency Cases

Emergency registration and treatment are supported under the scheme
for eligible listed therapies, with the hospital obtaining the
required authorisation according to scheme procedures.


## 8. Application / Treatment Process

Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva is not primarily a conventional online welfare
application.

### Normal Process

1. Beneficiary approaches a government facility, health camp,
   network hospital or relevant healthcare point.
2. Beneficiary identity is verified.
3. Medical condition is assessed.
4. If the disease/treatment is covered, the patient is referred/
   registered for the appropriate package.
5. Network hospital obtains required authorisation.
6. Treatment is provided cashlessly.
7. Discharge and post-discharge services are provided according to
   the approved package.
8. Claims are processed through the Trust's online system.

### Vaidya Mithra

Vaidya Mithras/help desks assist beneficiaries at relevant
healthcare facilities.

They help patients with:

- Eligibility verification
- Registration
- Scheme information
- Hospital process
- Documentation
- Grievances

## 10. Selection / Verification / Approval

There is no competitive selection process.

### Beneficiary Verification

Eligibility is established through the Andhra Pradesh government
beneficiary/Civil Supplies database.

The system identifies eligible BPL/White Ration Card beneficiaries.

### Treatment Verification

The hospital verifies:

- Beneficiary identity
- Eligibility
- Medical condition
- Covered therapy/procedure
- Package applicability

### Pre-Authorisation

Where required, the network hospital obtains authorisation through
the Trust's online processing system.

### Emergency

Emergency admission is available for eligible covered conditions,
with the hospital obtaining the required emergency authorisation.

## 12. Related / Linked Government Programs

### Aarogya Raksha

Aarogya Raksha is a separate healthcare programme for APL families
described in Andhra Pradesh government material.

It should not be merged with Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva.

### Employee Health Scheme (EHS)

EHS is a separate scheme for eligible government employees,
pensioners and dependants.

It should not be treated as the same beneficiary pool.

### Amrutha Health Scheme

Amrutha Health Scheme is a separate programme for eligible orphans
and destitutes in recognised institutions.

### Aarogya Aasara

Aarogya Aasara is a post-operative sustenance allowance associated
with eligible Vaidya Seva patients.

It is a related benefit and should be represented separately if
included in the RAG.

### Central Government Health Schemes

Central programmes such as Ayushman Bharat/PM-JAY have separate
eligibility and benefit rules.

Do not merge their eligibility rules with NTR Vaidya Seva.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva is a healthcare assurance scheme, not a
   direct cash-transfer programme.

2. Eligibility is strongly tied to the government beneficiary
   database.

3. Treatment must fall within the approved scheme package/list.

4. Treatment must be delivered through an authorised/empanelled
   healthcare provider where required.

5. The RAG must distinguish:
   beneficiary eligibility
   from
   treatment/package eligibility.

6. A person being an eligible beneficiary does not mean every medical
   procedure is automatically free.

7. A hospital being a healthcare facility does not automatically mean
   every service at that hospital is covered.

8. Current government sources expose different generations of
   financial/procedure figures.

9. The newer ₹5 lakh figure should be stored as the current district
   government value with provenance.

10. The older ₹2.5 lakh figure should be retained only as historical
    information.

11. Do not import eligibility criteria from NTR Bharosa, Thalliki
    Vandanam, Deepam 2.0 or other AP welfare schemes.

12. Do not claim universal coverage for every Andhra Pradesh resident.

13. The exact covered therapy list should be treated as a separately
    versioned dataset.

14. Current network-hospital availability should also be treated as
    dynamic data.
