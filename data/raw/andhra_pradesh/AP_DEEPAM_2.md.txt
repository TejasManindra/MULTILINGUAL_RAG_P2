## 2. Scheme Overview

Deepam 2.0 is an Andhra Pradesh Government scheme under which
eligible households receive three LPG cylinder refills free of cost
per year.

The scheme was launched in October 2024 and implementation began
from 01 November 2024.

The Civil Supplies Department implements the scheme through the
existing LPG distribution network.

Current Andhra Pradesh government district information states that
three LPG refills are provided free of cost to LPG consumers who
have Rice Cards.

The scheme is therefore linked to:

- An active LPG connection
- A valid Rice Card
- Aadhaar-linked/validated beneficiary information
- LPG, Rice Card and Aadhaar data validation

The benefit is delivered through the LPG booking and reimbursement/
subsidy mechanism rather than as an unrestricted cash transfer.


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

Primary beneficiary:

Eligible households/consumers in Andhra Pradesh who:

- Have a valid Rice Card
- Have an active LPG connection
- Satisfy the required Aadhaar/data-validation conditions
- Have their LPG connection and household information successfully
  validated in the government system

The scheme is household/consumer based.

The currently verified government material does not establish a
separate universal age requirement for the beneficiary.

The scheme should therefore not be encoded as an age-based welfare
scheme.


## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### Rice Card

The beneficiary must have a valid Rice Card.

Current Andhra Pradesh district Civil Supplies information explicitly
states that Deepam-2 is implemented for LPG consumers who have Rice
Cards.


### LPG Connection

The beneficiary must have an active LPG connection in Andhra Pradesh.

The LPG connection must be successfully validated against the
government beneficiary database.


### Aadhaar

Aadhaar information is part of the beneficiary validation process.

The LPG connection, Rice Card and Aadhaar information are matched/
validated with relevant government databases.

Do not represent Aadhaar merely as an optional supporting document
when describing the beneficiary-validation mechanism.


### Residence

The verified implementation material establishes the beneficiary
through Andhra Pradesh's Rice Card and LPG databases.

A separate standalone domicile-age-income test was not identified
in the verified current scheme material.

Therefore:

Residence:
Andhra Pradesh beneficiary/database eligibility is required;
no separate numerical domicile criterion identified.


### Income

No separate numerical household-income threshold was identified in
the verified current Deepam-2 implementation material.

Therefore:

Income limit:
NOT SPECIFIED.


### Occupation

No occupation requirement was identified.


### Education

No education requirement was identified.


### Land

No landholding requirement was identified.


### Social Category

No caste-specific eligibility condition was identified.

Eligibility is primarily linked to Rice Card, LPG connection and
database validation.


### Family Requirement

The benefit is administered at the household/eligible LPG-consumer
level.

The current state survey and government implementation material
describe the benefit as three LPG cylinders per beneficiary family
per year.


## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

The following conditions can prevent a beneficiary from receiving the
Deepam-2 benefit:

- No valid Rice Card
- No active/eligible LPG connection
- LPG connection not successfully validated
- Rice Card information not successfully validated
- Aadhaar/data mismatch preventing beneficiary validation

### Important

Do not add the following as Deepam-2 exclusions unless supported by
a specific current Government Order or implementation guideline:

- Specific annual household income ceiling
- Land ceiling
- Vehicle ownership ceiling
- Electricity consumption ceiling
- Property-size ceiling
- Employment in government service
- Caste exclusion

These conditions are used in some other Andhra Pradesh welfare
schemes but were not established as universal Deepam-2 eligibility
rules in the verified material.


## 6. Benefits

### Primary Benefit

Three free LPG cylinder refills per eligible beneficiary family
per year.

### Annual Frequency

Maximum benefit:

3 LPG refills per year.

### Refill Schedule

From FY 2025-26 onward, the scheme operates on three four-month
blocks:

Block I:
April – July

Block II:
August – November

Block III:
December – March

One eligible cylinder is allocated per block.

This means the normal annual structure is:

- 1 cylinder during April–July
- 1 cylinder during August–November
- 1 cylinder during December–March

### Initial 2024-25 Provision

For the initial implementation year, the first cylinder could be
booked during the extended period from 29 October 2024 through
31 March 2025.

This initial booking window should not be confused with the normal
four-month block schedule used from FY 2025-26 onward.


### Benefit Delivery

The beneficiary books the LPG cylinder through the normal LPG
distribution system.

After delivery, the eligible subsidy/reimbursement is processed
through the government mechanism.

Implementation material states that the subsidy amount is credited
to the beneficiary's bank account after delivery.

## 8. Application / Benefit Claim Process

Deepam 2.0 is primarily an entitlement/benefit-delivery scheme
rather than a conventional online welfare application.

### Normal Process

1. Beneficiary has a valid Rice Card.
2. Beneficiary has an active LPG connection.
3. LPG, Rice Card and Aadhaar information are validated.
4. Beneficiary books the eligible LPG cylinder.
5. LPG cylinder is delivered.
6. Eligible subsidy/reimbursement is processed.
7. Subsidy is credited to the beneficiary's bank account.

### KYC

KYC/data validation is important for beneficiaries whose LPG,
Aadhaar and Rice Card records are not already successfully matched.

Gas delivery personnel and local administration may facilitate
KYC/data correction activities.

### Grievance / Support

Government implementation material has identified the Civil Supplies
grievance mechanism and toll-free number 1967 for beneficiary
queries/complaints.

Beneficiaries may also approach the relevant local Secretariat /
Civil Supplies office where applicable.

Deepam 2.0 does not operate primarily through a competitive
beneficiary-selection process.

## 12. Related / Linked Government Programs

### Earlier Deepam Scheme

The original Deepam scheme provided LPG connections to eligible
BPL women/households.

Deepam 2.0 is a separate later programme providing recurring free
LPG refills.

Do not merge the two schemes into one RAG record.


### PM Ujjwala Yojana

PM Ujjwala Yojana is a Government of India LPG-connection scheme.

It is separate from Deepam 2.0.

A beneficiary may have an LPG connection through a central LPG
programme while state Deepam-2 eligibility depends on the applicable
Andhra Pradesh eligibility/validation rules.

Do not automatically treat PMUY eligibility as identical to
Deepam-2 eligibility.


### Public Distribution System / Rice Card

Rice Card eligibility is an important input to Deepam-2 beneficiary
identification.

The PDS/Rice Card system therefore acts as a linked government
database.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. Deepam 2.0 should be modelled as an LPG benefit scheme rather
   than a generic cash-transfer scheme.

2. The core benefit is three LPG refills per year.

3. The benefit is delivered through the LPG booking/distribution
   ecosystem and government subsidy/reimbursement mechanism.

4. Rice Card status is a key eligibility input.

5. Active LPG connection is a key eligibility input.

6. Aadhaar and cross-database validation are important to the
   beneficiary-verification process.

7. The initial 2024-25 booking window is historical and should not
   be used as the current annual deadline.

8. The April–July, August–November and December–March blocks apply
   to the regular annual structure from FY 2025-26.

9. Do not copy eligibility conditions from other Andhra Pradesh
   welfare schemes into Deepam 2.0.

10. Do not state that all women automatically qualify.

11. Do not state that every LPG consumer automatically qualifies.

12. Do not treat the earlier Deepam LPG-connection scheme as the
   same scheme.

13. PM Ujjwala Yojana is a separate Central Government programme.

14. Current beneficiary eligibility should be determined from the
   live Civil Supplies/LPG/Rice Card validation systems.
