## 2. Scheme Overview

Stree Shakti is an Andhra Pradesh Government free public-transport
scheme launched on 15 August 2025.

The original scheme provides free travel to:

- Girls
- Women
- Transgender persons

who have Andhra Pradesh domicile status and produce valid identity proof.

The original scheme operates in specified APSRTC bus services.

The Government Order specifies that the scheme is implemented across
Andhra Pradesh.

The scheme is intended to improve mobility and access to:

- Education
- Employment
- Healthcare
- Other essential activities

The Government reimburses APSRTC for the eligible zero-fare travel
provided under the scheme.

The scheme should be represented as a transport-benefit programme,
not as a cash-transfer scheme.


## 3. Target Beneficiaries

### Original Stree Shakti Beneficiaries

The original Government Order identifies:

- Girls
- Women
- Transgender individuals

with Andhra Pradesh domicile status.

A valid identity document must be produced.

There is no age restriction specified in the original Stree Shakti
Government Order for the general women/girls/transgender beneficiary
category.

### 2026 Disability-Travel Expansion

G.O.Ms.No.21 dated 28.02.2026 separately provides free travel for
eligible persons with disabilities covered under Section 2(zc) of
the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, with disability
of 40% or more, in Stree Shakti-covered buses.

Eligible escorts receive a 50% concession according to the applicable
disability-travel rules.

Important:
The disability provision is a separate 2026 transport expansion and
should not be used to redefine the original Stree Shakti women/girls/
transgender eligibility.


## 4. Eligibility Criteria

### Age

Original Stree Shakti scheme:

No general minimum or maximum age is specified in G.O.Ms.No.27.

The scheme uses the category "girls, women and transgender individuals"
rather than establishing a numerical age threshold.

Therefore:

Age:
No numerical age limit specified in the verified original Government
Order.


### Gender

Original beneficiaries:

- Girls
- Women
- Transgender individuals

The official APSRTC page describes the eligible population as girls,
women and transgender citizens domiciled in Andhra Pradesh.


### Residence

The beneficiary must have Andhra Pradesh domicile status.

This is an explicit condition in G.O.Ms.No.27.

The beneficiary must also produce valid identity proof.


### Income

No income limit is specified.

Stree Shakti is not described as an income-tested welfare programme.


### Occupation

No occupation requirement is specified.

Women/girls/transgender persons may use the facility regardless of
occupation, subject to domicile, identity and bus-service conditions.


### Education

No education requirement is specified.

The scheme is not restricted to students.


### Land

No landholding requirement is specified.


### Social Category

No caste/social-category restriction is specified.

The original scheme is available to eligible beneficiaries regardless
of social category, subject to domicile and identity requirements.


### Family Requirement

No family-income or family-size requirement is specified.

Eligibility is individual and travel-based rather than a household
cash-benefit calculation.


### Other Conditions

The beneficiary must:

- Have Andhra Pradesh domicile status.
- Produce valid identity proof.
- Travel in an APSRTC/APPTD service covered by Stree Shakti.
- Comply with applicable ticketing and conductor-verification
  procedures.


## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility

The original Stree Shakti Government Order excludes travel in:

- Non-stop services
- Interstate services
- Contract Carriage services
- Chartered services
- Package Tours
- Saptagiri Express
- Ultra Deluxe
- Super Luxury
- Star Liner
- AC products

The scheme is therefore not a blanket "all APSRTC buses are free"
programme.

### Service-specific exclusion

A person who is otherwise eligible for Stree Shakti must still pay
the applicable fare when travelling on services that are outside
the Stree Shakti coverage.

### Domicile

Persons without Andhra Pradesh domicile status are not eligible under
the original Stree Shakti scheme.

### Identity Proof

The beneficiary must produce valid identity proof.

Exact acceptable-ID list:
Not exhaustively specified in the Government Order reviewed.

The current APSRTC implementation should be followed for acceptable
identity verification.


## 6. Benefits

### Primary Benefit

Free bus travel / zero-fare travel.

Eligible beneficiaries receive zero-fare tickets on covered APSRTC
services.

### Covered Services

The original Government Order identifies five service categories:

1. Pallevelugu
2. Ultra Pallevelugu
3. City Ordinary
4. Metro Express
5. Express

### Enclave Routes

Eligible beneficiaries are permitted to travel free on enclave route
services.

### Travel Area

The original scheme is a statewide Andhra Pradesh transport scheme.

The beneficiary can travel within Andhra Pradesh on eligible covered
services.

Interstate services are excluded.

### Number of Journeys

No numerical daily/monthly journey limit is specified in the verified
Government Order.

Therefore:

Journey limit:
Not specified.

### Cash Benefit

There is no direct cash payment to beneficiaries.

The benefit is free transportation.

### Government Reimbursement

The Government reimburses APSRTC based on eligible zero-fare tickets
issued under the scheme.


## 7. Required Documents

The original Government Order requires:

- Valid identity proof.

The current APSRTC scheme page also states that eligible beneficiaries
must produce valid ID proof.

The exact list of acceptable identity documents should be taken from
the current APSRTC implementation system.

Potential identity documents may include government-issued identity
documents where accepted by APSRTC.

Do not mark Aadhaar as the only universally mandatory document unless
the current APSRTC implementation guideline explicitly states this.

No income certificate is required under the verified original scheme.

No caste certificate is required under the verified original scheme.

No land document is required under the verified original scheme.


## 8. Application Process

Stree Shakti is primarily a travel entitlement rather than a
cash-benefit application scheme.

### Normal Travel Process

1. Beneficiary travels on an eligible Stree Shakti service.
2. Beneficiary produces valid identity proof when required.
3. Conductor verifies eligibility/identity.
4. A zero-fare ticket is issued.
5. Beneficiary travels without paying the normal fare.

The Government Order directs APSRTC/APPTD to implement the scheme and
issue zero-fare tickets.

### Bus Pass / Registration

A separate advance registration or welfare-application process is not
established as a universal requirement in the original Government Order.

Exact current APSRTC ticketing/identity-verification workflow should
be followed.

### Government Reimbursement

APSRTC records eligible zero-fare tickets and claims reimbursement
from the Government according to the applicable administrative
process.

## 10. Selection / Verification / Approval

Stree Shakti does not use a conventional beneficiary selection process.

Eligibility is determined at the point of travel through:

- Domicile status
- Identity proof
- Covered service category

APSRTC conductors/operational staff verify the passenger according
to the applicable ticketing procedure.

### 2026 PwD Expansion

For persons with disabilities under the 2026 expansion:

- Disability must meet the applicable 40% threshold.
- Eligibility is linked to the applicable disability certification/
  SADAREM system.
- Eligible escorts receive 50% concession under the applicable rules.

The PwD expansion is administered separately from the original
women/girls/transgender eligibility.

## 17. Notes and Limitations

1. Stree Shakti is a transportation benefit, not a cash-transfer
   welfare scheme.

2. Eligibility for the original scheme is based primarily on:
   - AP domicile
   - beneficiary category
   - valid identity proof
   - eligible bus service.

3. The five covered bus categories must be treated as a hard service
   filter in the RAG.

4. Interstate services are excluded even though the beneficiary may
   otherwise qualify.

5. AC and specified premium services are excluded.

6. The 2026 PwD free-travel expansion should be represented as a
   separate rule/component.

7. The 2026 PwD expansion does not change the original Stree Shakti
   eligibility definition for women/girls/transgender beneficiaries.

8. No income or land eligibility condition should be added because
   none is specified in the verified original scheme sources.

9. No cash amount should be displayed as a beneficiary benefit.

10. The benefit is zero-fare transportation.

11. Final operational ticketing/identity-verification procedures are
    subject to APSRTC's current implementation system.
