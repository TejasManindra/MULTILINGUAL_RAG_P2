# CROP LOAN WAIVER SCHEME-2024 (RUNA MAFEE)

## 1. Basic Information
Scheme Name: Crop Loan Waiver Scheme-2024
Official Scheme Name: Telangana Crop Loan Waiver Scheme 2024
Scheme ID: G.O. Rt. No. 567 (dated 15/07/2024)
State: Telangana
Government: Government of Telangana
Department: Department of Agriculture and Co-operation
Implementing Agency: Banks and Agriculture Department
Category: Agriculture
Subcategory: Debt Relief / Loan Waiver
Scheme Status: ACTIVE
Launch Date: July 15, 2024
Current Version / Notification: Ongoing through 2026.

## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Alleviate debt burden on farmers from short-term crop loans.
Description: Pays off outstanding short-term crop loans (principal + interest) taken between Dec 2018 and Dec 2023.
Objectives: Free farmers from debt cycle to secure fresh credit.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Farmers holding outstanding short-term crop loans.
Who the scheme is intended for: Resident farmers.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Occupation Requirement: Farmer.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Possess documented agricultural land details.
Social Category Requirement: All categories.
Family Requirement: Applied per family based on Food Security Card (Ration Card).
Other Eligibility Conditions: Loan availed between Dec 11, 2018 and Dec 9, 2023; Short-term crop loan; MUST possess Ration Card.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Long-term agricultural loans.
- Loans outside the specified date window.
- Farmers without a Food Security Card.
- Unregistered gold loans.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Complete waiver of agricultural debt.
Financial Assistance: Direct settlement to bank.
Benefit Amount: Up to ₹2,00,000 (Principal + Interest) maximum per family.
Frequency: One-time.
Duration: Applies to historical loan window.
Other Benefits: Restoration of creditworthiness.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: Aadhaar Card. Purpose: Identity. Source: Suryapet Portal.
Document: Food Security Card (Ration Card). Purpose: Define family unit. Source: GO 567.
Document: Bank/Loan Details & Land details. Purpose: Verification. Source: Suryapet Portal.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Offline grievance / Data sync.
Online / Offline: Hybrid.
Application Portal: clw.telangana.gov.in
Where to Apply: Mandal Agriculture Officers (MAO).
Step-by-Step Process: Data auto-fetched from banks -> Check status on portal -> If missing, submit grievance with Ration Card/Loan proof to MAO -> Committee reviews -> Funds transferred to bank.
