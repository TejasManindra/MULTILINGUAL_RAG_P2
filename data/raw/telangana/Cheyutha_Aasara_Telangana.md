# CHEYUTHA SCHEME (FORMERLY AASARA PENSIONS)

## 1. Basic Information
Scheme Name: Cheyutha Scheme (historically Aasara Pensions)
Official Scheme Name: Telangana Cheyutha Scheme
Scheme ID: Not specified in the verified source.
State: Telangana
Government: Government of Telangana
Department: Department of Rural Development, SERP
Implementing Agency: District Rural Development Agency (DRDA)
Category: Social Welfare / Pensions
Subcategory: Financial Assistance
Scheme Status: ACTIVE
Launch Date: November 2014 (Updated 2023/2024)
Current Version / Notification: Cheyutha Scheme Guidelines 2026.

## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: To alleviate financial burdens on economically weaker sections.
Description: Delivers direct monthly financial assistance to diverse vulnerable categories alongside linked healthcare coverage.
Objectives: To ensure a secured life with dignity for the poor.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Senior citizens, widows, disabled individuals, single women, beedi workers, handloom weavers, toddy tappers, and specific disease patients.
Who the scheme is intended for: Below Poverty Line (BPL) individuals.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Old Age: 57+; Widows: 18+; Single Women: 30+ (Rural) / 35+ (Urban); Weavers/Tappers: 50+; Disabled: No limit.
Gender Requirement: Specific to category.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: Below Poverty Line (BPL) holding a White Ration Card.
Occupation Requirement: Category specific (Weavers, Beedi workers).
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: All categories meeting BPL criteria.
Family Requirement: Only one OAP beneficiary permitted per household.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Disabled must have >40% disability via SADAREM.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Non-BPL families without a White Ration Card.
- Secondary elderly members in a household for OAP.
- Unregistered practitioners (for weavers/toddy tappers).

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Monthly Direct Financial Assistance.
Financial Assistance: Direct Benefit Transfer.
Benefit Amount: ₹4,000/month (Standard categories); ₹6,000/month (Disabled).
Frequency: Monthly.
Duration: Continuous.
Other Benefits: ₹10 lakh health insurance under Rajiv Aarogyasri.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: White Ration Card. Purpose: Proof of BPL. Source: Warangal Portal.
Document: Category Proofs (Death Cert, SADAREM). Purpose: Prove specific sub-category eligibility. Source: DRDA SERP.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Offline.
Online / Offline: Offline with online tracking.
Application Portal: cheyutha.telangana.gov.in
Where to Apply: Gram Panchayat or MPDO Office via Praja Palana.
Step-by-Step Process: Apply offline, undergo physical verification, DRDA approves, and DBT is initiated.
