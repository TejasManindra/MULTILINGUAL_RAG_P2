## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: To empower women by reducing daily living and mobility costs.
Description: Provides completely free state-run bus travel for women and highly subsidized domestic LPG gas cylinders (₹500).
Objectives: Enhance women's mobility, workforce participation, and reduce financial stress.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Women, girls, and transgender persons.
Who the scheme is intended for: Female residents (and BPL families for gas).
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Adult for gas connection; No restriction for buses.
Gender Requirement: Female and Transgender.
Residence Requirement: Resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: No limit for bus; Gas requires BPL/income below ₹2 lakh.
Occupation Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: All categories.
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Must possess a local Aadhaar (Bus); Active gas connection + White Ration Card (Gas).

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Free Bus Travel: Male passengers, inter-state routes.
- Subsidized Gas: Households earning above ₹2 lakh, Non-BPL.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Free transport and fuel subsidy.
Financial Assistance: Direct subsidy.
Benefit Amount: Free bus tickets; LPG cylinders at ₹500.
Frequency: Unlimited (Bus); One per month (Gas).
Duration: Continuous.
Other Benefits: None specified.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: Aadhaar Card. Purpose: Domicile for bus. Source: Mahabubnagar Portal.
Document: BPL Ration Card & Gas Receipt. Purpose: Gas subsidy. Source: Mahabubnagar Portal.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Offline / Point-of-service.
Online / Offline: Offline submission.
Application Portal: Praja Palana tracking.
Where to Apply: Show ID in bus; Apply for gas at Praja Palana Seva Kendram.
Step-by-Step Process:
1. Bus: Present Aadhaar to conductor for a Zero Ticket.
2. Gas: Submit form with Ration Card and Gas Receipt at Panchayat office.
