# KALYANA LAKSHMI SCHEME (TELANGANA)

## 1. Basic Information
Scheme Name: Kalyana Lakshmi
Official Scheme Name: Kalyana Lakshmi Pathakam
Scheme ID: Not specified in the verified source.
State: Telangana
Government: Government of Telangana
Department: SC/ST/BC Welfare Departments
Implementing Agency: Revenue Divisional Officers (RDOs) / Tahsildars
Category: Social Welfare
Subcategory: Financial Assistance for Marriage
Scheme Status: ACTIVE
Launch Date: Oct 2, 2014
Current Version / Notification: Active with ₹1,00,116 limit.

## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Prevent child marriages and alleviate marriage expense burdens on poor families.
Description: One-time direct financial grant to the bride's family at marriage.
Objectives: Empower women, encourage education (delaying marriage to 18).

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Unmarried girls from BPL SC, ST, BC, EBC families.
Who the scheme is intended for: Poor families of the bride.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Bride must have completed 18 years at marriage.
Gender Requirement: Female.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: SC/ST: ≤ ₹2,00,000; BC/EBC: ≤ ₹2,00,000 (Urban) / ₹1,50,000 (Rural).
Occupation Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: SC, ST, BC, EBC.
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Below Poverty Line (BPL).

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Brides below 18 years.
- Families exceeding income thresholds.
- Minority communities (Covered under Shaadi Mubarak).

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Financial assistance for marriage.
Financial Assistance: Direct Bank Transfer.
Benefit Amount: ₹1,00,116.
Frequency: One-time.
Duration: Single disbursement.
Other Benefits: None specified.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: Age Proof (DOB). Purpose: Verify age 18+. Source: Official Portal.
Document: Income/Caste Cert. Purpose: Financial/Social eligibility. Source: MeeSeva.
Document: Aadhaar, Wedding Card, Bank Passbook. Purpose: ID, Event proof, DBT.

## 8. Application Process

Online / Offline: Online + Physical check.
Application Portal: telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in
Where to Apply: Online.
Step-by-Step Process: Apply online before/shortly after marriage -> RDO conducts physical field verification -> Funds transferred via DBT.
