## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Extend financial security to agricultural laborers who do not own farming land.
Description: A distinct, linked sub-program providing a flat annual financial grant to landless farm workers.
Objectives: Ensure equitable support reaches the lowest rung of the agricultural workforce.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Landless agricultural laborers.
Who the scheme is intended for: Farm workers relying on agricultural labor without possessing land.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Occupation Requirement: Agricultural laborer.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Must be landless.
Social Category Requirement: All categories.
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Landowning farmers / Pattadars (Covered under Rythu Bharosa).
- Non-agricultural workers.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Annual financial grant.
Financial Assistance: Direct Benefit Transfer.
Benefit Amount: ₹12,000 annually. (Flat rate, not per acre).
Frequency: Annual.
Duration: Continuous.
