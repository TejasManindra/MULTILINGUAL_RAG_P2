# RYTHU BIMA SCHEME (TELANGANA)

## 1. Basic Information
Scheme Name: Rythu Bima
Official Scheme Name: Rythu Bandhu Farmer Group Life Insurance Scheme
Scheme ID: Not specified in the verified source.
State: Telangana
Government: Government of Telangana
Department: Department of Agriculture
Implementing Agency: Agriculture Dept / LIC
Category: Social Security / Welfare
Subcategory: Life Insurance for Farmers
Scheme Status: ACTIVE
Launch Date: August 14, 2018
Current Version / Notification: Active for 2025-2026 cycle.

## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Provide immediate financial relief to families of deceased farmers.
Description: 100% government-funded group life insurance. Sum assured transferred to nominee within 10 days of farmer's death.
Objectives: Prevent dependent families from facing severe financial crises.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Landowning farmers and RoFR title holders.
Who the scheme is intended for: Small/marginal farmers whose livelihood is agriculture.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: 18 to 59 years.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Occupation Requirement: Farmer.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Agricultural land ownership (capped up to 5 acres for fresh enrollments); valid Pattadar/RoFR title.
Social Category Requirement: All categories.
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Not specified in the verified source.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Tenant farmers.
- Farmers aged 60 and above.
- Individuals without formal land records.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Life Insurance Coverage.
Financial Assistance: Direct cash payout to nominee.
Benefit Amount: ₹5,00,000.
Frequency: One-time payment.
Duration: Annual coverage (Auto-renewed).
Other Benefits: Zero premium burden.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: Pattadar Passbook / RoFR Title. Purpose: Proof of land. Source: District Portal.
Document: Death Certificate. Purpose: Mandatory for claim. Source: Claim Procedures.
Document: Claim Form & Aadhaar. Purpose: Nominee submission. Source: Claim Procedures.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Offline Claims.
Online / Offline: Hybrid.
Application Portal: rythubandhu.telangana.gov.in
Where to Apply: Nominees submit claim at local AEO or LIC.
Step-by-Step Process: Master enrollment via land records. On death, nominee gets death certificate -> Submits claim form to AEO -> LIC processes payout within 10 days.
