Current Version / Notification: September 2026 screening guidelines.

## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Facilitate international higher education for meritorious marginalized students.
Description: Grant of up to ₹20 Lakhs, airfare, and visa fees for approved foreign universities.
Objectives: Promote global exposure and capacity building.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Graduates aiming for PG/Ph.D. abroad.
Who the scheme is intended for: SC, ST, BC, EBC, Minority students.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Below 35 years.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: Family income < ₹5,00,000 annually.
Occupation Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Education Requirement: Minimum 60% in Graduation.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: SC, ST, BC, EBC, Minority.
Family Requirement: Only one child per family.
Other Eligibility Conditions: GRE(260)/GMAT(500)/TOEFL(60)/IELTS(6.0)/PTE(50); Unconditional admission (I-20/COE) in 10 designated countries.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- UG studies abroad.
- Second child from same family.
- Conditional admissions.
- Unapproved countries.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Financial grant for overseas tuition/living.
Financial Assistance: Grant in installments.
Benefit Amount: Up to ₹20,00,000.
Frequency: Two installments.
Duration: Course duration.
Other Benefits: One-way flight ticket, Visa fees.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: Passport, Visa, I-20/COE. Purpose: Travel/Admission proof. Source: ePASS.
Document: GRE/IELTS Scorecards. Purpose: Proficiency proof. Source: ePASS.
Document: Income/Caste (< ₹5L). Purpose: Eligibility. Source: MeeSeva.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Online with physical biometric.
Online / Offline: Online via ePASS + State Screening.
Application Portal: telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in/OverseasLinks.do
Where to Apply: Online.
Step-by-Step Process: Secure Visa/I-20 -> Register on ePASS -> Upload docs -> Biometric auth -> State Level Screening Committee review -> Disbursement.

## 9. Important Dates
Application Start: Bi-annual intakes (Jan/Aug).
Application End: Strict deadlines per intake (e.g., Sept 30, 2026).
Deadline: Monitored via portal.
Financial Year: 2026-2027.

## 10. Selection / Verification / Approval
Selection Criteria: Merit (60% Grad, 20% GRE, 20% English).
Verification Process: Document review -> Screening Committee meetings.
Approval Authority: State Level Screening Committee.
Payment / Benefit Delivery Method: Direct Bank Transfer (DBT).
