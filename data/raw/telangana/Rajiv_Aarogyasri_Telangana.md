# RAJIV AAROGYASRI (TELANGANA)

## 1. Basic Information
Scheme Name: Rajiv Aarogyasri
Official Scheme Name: Rajiv Aarogyasri Community Health Insurance Scheme
Scheme ID: Not specified in the verified source.
State: Telangana
Government: Government of Telangana
Department: Department of Health, Medical & Family Welfare
Implementing Agency: Aarogyasri Health Care Trust (AHCT)
Category: Health Insurance
Subcategory: Cashless Medical Treatment
Scheme Status: ACTIVE
Launch Date: 2007 (Upgraded limits in 2024)
Current Version / Notification: ₹10 Lakh upgraded coverage (2026).

## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Ensure financial constraints do not hinder access to critical healthcare.
Description: Comprehensive, end-to-end cashless medical services and surgeries for identified diseases at empanelled hospitals.
Objectives: To cater to the healthcare needs of the BPL population and prevent catastrophic health expenditures.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: BPL Families.
Who the scheme is intended for: Poor and vulnerable households requiring medical care.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
Occupation Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: All categories (subject to BPL status).
Family Requirement: Coverage is calculated per family.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Must possess a valid White Ration Card.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Residents outside of Telangana.
- Non-BPL families.
- Unapproved scheme packages/treatments.
- Treatments initiated before pre-authorisation is officially approved.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Cashless medical treatment.
Financial Assistance: Direct payment to empanelled hospitals.
Benefit Amount: Up to ₹10 lakh per family per year.
Frequency: Per-admission until limit is exhausted.
Duration: Lifetime validity of the card, renewed annually.
Other Benefits: Covers pre-existing conditions, organ transplants, and follow-up packages.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: White Ration Card. Purpose: BPL status. Source: Bajaj Finserv.
Document: Aadhaar Card. Purpose: Identity check. Source: Aarogyasri Mithra.
Document: Aarogyasri Health Card. Purpose: Hospital access. Source: Official Portal.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Hospital-based.
Online / Offline: Hybrid.
Application Portal: rajivaarogyasri.telangana.gov.in
Where to Apply: Village Secretariats or Aarogyasri Mithra desks at hospitals.
Step-by-Step Process: Visit empanelled hospital -> Submit cards to help desk -> Hospital generates pre-authorization -> Trust approves -> Cashless treatment provided.
