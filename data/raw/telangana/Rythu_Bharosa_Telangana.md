## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: To provide timely working capital directly to farmers at the beginning of each crop cycle (Kharif and Rabi).
Description: The scheme aims to cover the costs of seeds, fertilizers, and field labor by transferring funds via DBT. It replaces the former Rythu Bandhu program.
Objectives: To ensure agricultural investment support reaches genuine cultivators, including tenant farmers, while excluding real estate and fallow lands.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Small and marginal landowning farmers, registered tenant farmers, ROFR landholders.
Who the scheme is intended for: Active cultivators.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Gender Requirement: All Genders.
Residence Requirement: Must be a permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: Income Tax (ITR) payers are excluded.
Occupation Requirement: Small/Marginal Farmer, Tenant Farmer.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Must possess active, cultivable agricultural land fully registered in the state revenue portal (Bhu Bharati/Dharani).
Social Category Requirement: All categories (ROFR landholders specifically included).
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Must have an active bank account linked to Aadhaar.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Non-cultivable plots.
- Real estate layouts and industrial zones.
- Fallow lands, rocky terrains, and hillocks.
- Income Tax (ITR) filers.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Agricultural working capital.
Financial Assistance: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
Benefit Amount: ₹12,000 per acre, annually. Paddy farmers get an additional bonus of ₹500 per quintal. Landless laborers receive ₹12,000/year under the linked sub-program.
Frequency: Bi-annual (Kharif and Rabi installments of ₹6,000 each).
Duration: Continuous.
Other Benefits: Linked to Rythu Bima life insurance.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: Aadhaar Card & Voter ID. Purpose: Identity and Age proof. Source: Vikaspedia.
Document: Ration Card & Bank Passbook. Purpose: Economic status and DBT routing. Source: Vikaspedia.
Document: Agricultural Land Documents / Valid Lease. Purpose: Proof of cultivable land or tenancy. Source: Mulugu District Portal.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Online or Offline.
Online / Offline: Hybrid.
Application Portal: Praja Palana Website / Official Agriculture Department Portal.
Where to Apply: Praja Palana Centre or Gram Sabha/Panchayat Office.
Step-by-Step Process:
1. Farmers visit a Praja Palana Centre or Gram Sabha.
2. Fill out the application and attach required documents (land records, Aadhaar).
3. The Village Development Officer verifies the application locally.
4. Forwarded to the District Agriculture Officer for final approval.
5. Funds are deposited via DBT.
