## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Reduce the cost of living for lower-income households.
Description: Offers free electricity up to 200 units per month to eligible BPL households, resulting in a "Zero Bill".
Objectives: Ensure energy access for the poor.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Low-income group households.
Who the scheme is intended for: Below poverty line (BPL) domestic power consumers.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: Low-income group / Below Poverty Line.
Occupation Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Family Requirement: One connection per household.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Must possess a White Ration Card; Domestic residential connections only.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Commercial/Industrial connections.
- Non-BPL households without White Ration Cards.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Free domestic electricity.
Financial Assistance: Bill waiver.
Benefit Amount: Free electricity up to 200 units per month.
Frequency: Monthly billing cycle.
Duration: Continuous.
Other Benefits: None specified.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: White Ration Card. Purpose: BPL status. Source: Peddapalli Portal.
Document: Electricity Service Number. Purpose: Meter linkage. Source: Application requirement.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Offline via Praja Palana.
Online / Offline: Offline.
Application Portal: Praja Palana tracking.
Where to Apply: Gram Panchayat or Praja Palana counters.
Step-by-Step Process: Apply with Ration Card and USC Number -> DISCOM verifies against Civil Supplies data -> Next billing cycle reflects Zero Bill.
