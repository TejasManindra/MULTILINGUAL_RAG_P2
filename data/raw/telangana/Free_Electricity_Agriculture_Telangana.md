# FREE ELECTRICITY FOR AGRICULTURE (TELANGANA)

## 1. Basic Information
Scheme Name: Free Electricity for Agriculture
Official Scheme Name: 24x7 Free Power for Agriculture
Scheme ID: Not specified in the verified source.
State: Telangana
Government: Government of Telangana
Department: Energy Department
Implementing Agency: TGSPDCL / TGNPDCL
Category: Agriculture / Utilities
Subcategory: Electricity Subsidy
Scheme Status: ACTIVE
Launch Date: Jan 1, 2018
Current Version / Notification: Continuous as of 2026.

## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Support agricultural production and ensure uninterrupted irrigation.
Description: 24 hours of uninterrupted, completely free electricity supply to all agricultural pump sets and borewells.
Objectives: Eliminate power cost for irrigation, increase crop yields.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Farmers relying on groundwater/lift irrigation.
Who the scheme is intended for: Farmers with agricultural connections.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: No income limit.
Occupation Requirement: Farmer.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Possess cultivable agricultural land.
Social Category Requirement: All categories.
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Must have authorized Agricultural Electricity Service Connection.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Commercial/Industrial connections.
- Domestic connections (Gruha Jyothi applies).
- Illegal connections.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: 100% subsidy on agricultural electricity.
Financial Assistance: Direct waiver of tariffs.
Benefit Amount: Zero cost for 24x7 electricity supply.
Frequency: Continuous.
Duration: Indefinite.
Other Benefits: Reliable 3-phase power.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: Agricultural Electricity Connection / Land Passbook. Purpose: Establish tariff bracket. Source: TSNPDCL portal.

## 8. Application Process
Application Method: Online / Offline.
Online / Offline: Hybrid.
Application Portal: Mee-Seva or DISCOM portals.
Where to Apply: Mee-Seva (for new connections).
Step-by-Step Process: Auto-applied to existing meters. New meters require applying via Mee-Seva with land proofs -> pay initial setup -> service released as free power.
