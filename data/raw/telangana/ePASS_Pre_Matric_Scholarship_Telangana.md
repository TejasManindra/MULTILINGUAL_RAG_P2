## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Reduce school drop-out rates for marginalized communities.
Description: Annual maintenance stipend to students in Classes 5 to 10 in recognized govt/aided schools.
Objectives: Prevent early workforce entry and support basic education costs.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: School students from Classes 5 to 10.
Who the scheme is intended for: SC, ST, BC, EBC, Minority, and Disabled students.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: School age.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Domicile of Telangana.
Income Requirement: SC/ST ≤ ₹2L; BC/Minority (Urban) ≤ ₹2L; BC/Minority (Rural) ≤ ₹1.5L; EBC/Disabled ≤ ₹1L.
Occupation Requirement: Student.
Education Requirement: Classes 5 to 10.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: SC, ST, BC, EBC, Minority, Disabled.
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: 75% attendance.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Unrecognized private institutions.
- Income exceeds threshold.
- Attendance below 75%.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Stipend for books and maintenance.
Financial Assistance: Direct bank transfer.
Benefit Amount: Classes 5-8: ₹100-₹150/month. Classes 9-10: ₹750-₹1000 annually + ₹150-₹350/month.
Frequency: Annual/Monthly.
Duration: Renewable yearly until Class 10.
Other Benefits: None specified.

## 7. Required Documents
Document: Aadhaar Card. Purpose: Authentication. Source: ePASS.
Document: Income/Caste Certificate. Purpose: Eligibility. Source: MeeSeva.
Document: Bonafide & Passbook. Purpose: Enrollment & DBT. Source: Trackers.
