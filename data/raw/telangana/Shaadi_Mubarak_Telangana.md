Current Version / Notification: Active with ₹1,00,116 limit.

## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Financial assistance to poor Minority families for daughters' marriages.
Description: Sister scheme to Kalyana Lakshmi, offering a direct, one-time cash grant to minority brides.
Objectives: Prevent child marriages and ensure financial security.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Unmarried girls from Minority communities.
Who the scheme is intended for: Poor families of minority brides.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Completed 18 years of age.
Gender Requirement: Female.
Residence Requirement: Resident of Telangana State.
Income Requirement: Combined parental income ≤ ₹2,00,000.
Occupation Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: Minority Community only.
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Below Poverty Line.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Brides below 18 years.
- Families > ₹2,00,000 income.
- Non-minority communities.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Financial assistance for marriage.
Financial Assistance: Direct Bank Transfer (DBT).
Benefit Amount: ₹1,00,116.
Frequency: One-time payment.
Duration: Single disbursement.
Other Benefits: None specified.
