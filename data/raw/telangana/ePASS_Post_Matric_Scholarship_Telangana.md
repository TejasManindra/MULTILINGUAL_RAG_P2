## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Financial assistance and tuition fee reimbursement for marginalized students pursuing higher education.
Description: Facilitates direct reimbursement of tuition fees (RTF) to colleges and maintenance fees (MTF) to students' bank accounts for Class 11 up to Ph.D.
Objectives: Ensure marginalized students are not denied higher education due to financial constraints.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: Students pursuing post-matric courses.
Who the scheme is intended for: SC, ST, BC, EBC, Minority, and Disabled students.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Domicile of Telangana.
Income Requirement: SC/ST ≤ ₹2.5L; BC/EBC/Minority (Urban) ≤ ₹2L; BC/EBC/Minority (Rural) ≤ ₹1.5L; Disabled ≤ ₹1L.
Occupation Requirement: Student.
Education Requirement: Enrolled in recognized Post-Matric course.
Land Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Social Category Requirement: SC, ST, BC, EBC, Minority, Disabled.
Family Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Other Eligibility Conditions: 75% attendance mandatory.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Management Quota or spot admissions.
- Part-time or online courses.
- EBC students pursuing Intermediate courses.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Tuition Fee Reimbursement (RTF) and Maintenance Fee (MTF).
Financial Assistance: Direct bank transfer.
Benefit Amount: 100% RTF for SC/ST/Specific ranks; up to ₹35,000 for others. MTF up to ₹20,000 annually.
Frequency: Annual/Quarterly.
Duration: Course duration.
Other Benefits: None specified.
