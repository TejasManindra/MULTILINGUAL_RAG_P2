## 2. Scheme Overview
Purpose: Ensure affordable housing for the homeless and low-income populations.
Description: Provides financial assistance to poor families to construct pucca houses. Landless get a free plot + funds. Special plots for statehood activists.
Objectives: Eradicate homelessness among EWS.

## 3. Target Beneficiaries
Target Beneficiary: EWS, Homeless Families, Telangana Movement Fighters.
Who the scheme is intended for: Low-income families living in kutcha houses or landless.
Geographical Coverage: Telangana state.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
Age Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Gender Requirement: All genders.
Residence Requirement: Permanent resident of Telangana.
Income Requirement: EWS / Low-income group.
Occupation Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Education Requirement: Not specified in the verified source.
Land Requirement: Landless (L2) get plot + funds. Small plot owners (L1) get funds only.
Social Category Requirement: All eligible EWS categories.
Family Requirement: Must not already own a permanent house anywhere in India.
Other Eligibility Conditions: Activist quota requires proof of Telangana Movement participation.

## 5. Exclusions / Non-Eligibility
- Applicants who already own a permanent (pucca) house in India (L3 Category).
- High-income households / Non-EWS families.

## 6. Benefits
Main Benefit: Housing construction assistance.
Financial Assistance: Direct cash grant in installments.
Benefit Amount: ₹5,00,000 for construction costs. (Landless get a free plot as well). Activists get a 250 sq. yard plot.
Frequency: Released in phased installments linked to construction stages.
Duration: One-time grant.
Other Benefits: Infrastructure linkage.
